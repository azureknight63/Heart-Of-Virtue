# Anvil — Character Profile

## Overview
- **Name:** Anvil
- **Species:** Scree Strider (see `docs/lore/creatures/scree-strider.md`)
- **Age:** Approximately 6 years
- **Role:** Pack animal for *Iron & Oath* — hauls the anvil, weapon racks, armor
  stock, and canvas stalls whenever the Nomad Camp seasonally relocates
- **Bonded To:** Vespera (raised him from a chick; his handler in every
  practical sense)
- **Tolerated By:** Kaelen (co-owner in name; Anvil has never fully warmed to him)

Anvil is Kaelen and Vespera's Scree Strider, stationed at the Iron & Oath
stall in the Nomad Camp Tradepost. He is not a mount and not a fighter — he
is cargo infrastructure with a temper, and the reason the stall's entire
stock survives the camp's seasonal teardown-and-relocate along the river
route intact.

---

## Physical Appearance and Manner

Anvil is a heavy-boned Scree Strider, dust-brown and ochre in his mottled
plumage, standing taller than Kaelen at the shoulder when his neck is
raised. Years of hauling have thickened his already deep chest further
still, and his splayed, calloused feet show the wear of a thousand river
crossings and switchback descents. A harness of oiled leather straps runs
across his back and flanks, anchoring the crated tool stock and folded
canvas that make up half of Iron & Oath's traveling inventory.

He is usually found standing at the edge of the stall, head lowered toward
whatever forage he's found, one eye tracking the foot traffic drifting
through the Tradepost. His stillness is deceptive — the quill ridge along
his spine lies flat when he's at ease, but it rises the instant someone
gets careless near the harness straps.

---

## Personality and Disposition

- **Stubborn:** Anvil moves at exactly the pace he intends to move at, and
  no faster. Kaelen has given up trying to hurry him.
- **Protective of the Tool Crates:** The harness load is, as far as Anvil is
  concerned, his to guard. He tolerates Vespera and Kaelen handling it
  without complaint. Anyone else reaching for a strap gets a warning hiss
  and a raised quill ridge before anything else happens.
- **Fond of Vespera, Specifically:** Vespera raised Anvil from a chick after
  a wild clutch was scattered by a Talus Hound pack on the eastern slope —
  she was the one who fed him by hand through that first difficult season,
  and he has never stopped treating her as the one voice that overrides his
  own stubbornness. He leans into her without being asked. He does not do
  this for anyone else.
- **Merely Tolerant of Kaelen:** Kaelen named him — a joke about the one
  thing in the stall that doesn't move for anybody, made before Anvil proved
  he could be just as immovable as his namesake. Anvil has apparently never
  forgiven him for it. He'll accept Kaelen's handling without incident. He
  will not lean into Kaelen. Kaelen brings it up more than Anvil seems to
  care about.
- **Placid Under Load, Sharp Under Threat:** True to the species' don't-run
  instinct, Anvil doesn't spook. He plants his feet and raises his ridge.
  This makes him steady on the trail and genuinely unpleasant to press if he
  decides something is a real threat to the load he's carrying.

---

## Relationship with Kaelen & Vespera

Anvil is a fixture of Iron & Oath in the same way the anvil itself is — load-
bearing, permanent, and taken for granted by the couple in the specific way
you take for granted something that has never once let you down.

- **Vespera's Charge:** Vespera checks his harness straps and feet at the
  start and end of every relocation, the same methodical inspection she
  gives armor fittings. Anvil holds still for this the way he holds still
  for nothing else.
- **Kaelen's Running Joke:** Kaelen refers to Anvil as "the other stubborn
  fixture around here" and has long since stopped expecting affection in
  return. He respects the animal's judgment about strangers more than he
  lets on — if Anvil's ridge goes up at someone, Kaelen notices.
- **Shared Dependence:** Neither Kaelen nor Vespera says it directly, but
  Iron & Oath's mobility — the fact that it can tear down and rebuild
  somewhere new along the river route every season — depends entirely on
  Anvil making the crossing without losing the stock. He is not sentimental
  scenery. He is the stall's supply chain.

## Role in the Story & Gameplay

- **Ambient Tradepost Fixture:** Present at (4, 3) Tradepost alongside Kaelen
  and Vespera. Non-hostile, non-combatant — cannot be drawn into combat.
- **Interaction Flavor:** Responds to `talk`/inspection and `pet` with
  narrated gesture and sound only (no speech — see
  `docs/lore/creatures/scree-strider.md` for the species' communication
  profile). Reactions vary depending on whether the narration is framed
  around Vespera, Kaelen, or Anvil alone, reflecting the asymmetry in his
  bond to each of them.
- **World-Building Node:** A concrete, discoverable answer to how a mobile
  forge-and-armor stall manages to relocate along a route that defeats
  ordinary pack animals — see `docs/lore/creatures/scree-strider-history.md`
  for the full domestication history and its tie to the river route's
  viability as a trade corridor.
