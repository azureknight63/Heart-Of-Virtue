# Anvil — Character Profile

## Overview
- **Name:** Anvil
- **Species:** Shell-back (see `docs/lore/creatures/shell-back.md`)
- **Age:** Decades old — older than either Kaelen or Vespera, and long-since
  outlived whichever nomad first worked with him
- **Role:** Pack animal for *Iron & Oath* — hauls the anvil, weapon racks,
  armor stock, and canvas stalls whenever the Nomad Camp seasonally
  relocates. Travels the route alone, sent ahead of the human caravan with
  the heaviest gear
- **Bonded To:** Vespera, through years of consistent care routine (see
  "Personality and Disposition" below) — not a childhood bond, since
  Shell-backs don't imprint the way a hatchling does
- **Tolerated By:** Kaelen (co-owner in name; the routine has never fully
  transferred to him)

Anvil is Kaelen and Vespera's Shell-back, stationed at the Iron & Oath stall
in the Nomad Camp Tradepost. He is not a mount and not a fighter — he is
cargo infrastructure with a temper, and the reason the stall's entire stock
survives the camp's seasonal teardown-and-relocate along the river route
intact, unescorted, every single time.

---

## Physical Appearance and Manner

Anvil is a low, heavy-bodied Shell-back, his shell banded grey and
rust-ochre in layered striations that make him look, at rest, like a chunk
of the scarp itself. Decades of the same load riding the same spot have worn
smooth grooves into the shell's terraced plates, and the crated tool stock
and folded canvas ride lashed directly into them — a rig that looks
purpose-built because, at this point, it effectively is. Where the hatch
seals the cargo cavity, Kaelen fitted an iron collar and hinged latch years
ago, so the seal reads like a strongbox lid rather than an animal's own
anatomy.

He is usually found at the edge of the stall, foot planted, sensory stalks
tracking the foot traffic drifting through the Tradepost. His stillness is
deceptive — a stranger's hand anywhere near the latch gets a sharp hiss of
vented air before anything else happens.

## Personality and Disposition

- **Stubborn:** Anvil moves at exactly the pace he intends to move at, and
  no faster. Kaelen gave up trying to hurry him decades before Jean arrived.
- **Protective of the Hatch:** The sealed cargo cavity is, as far as Anvil
  is concerned, his to guard. He tolerates Vespera and Kaelen working the
  latch without complaint. Anyone else reaching for it gets a warning hiss
  and, if they persist, a full withdrawal — hatch sealed, nothing left to
  argue with.
- **Fond of Vespera, Specifically:** Anvil doesn't remember a childhood
  bond — Shell-backs don't form one — but he has spent years recognizing
  Vespera by scent and by the exact, repeated routine of her care: how she
  cleans his plates, checks the hatch seal, and feeds him, in the same order,
  every time. That recognition is what settles him. He has never fully
  settled for anyone else, including Kaelen.
- **Merely Tolerant of Kaelen:** Kaelen named him — a joke about the one
  thing in the stall that doesn't move for anybody, made before Anvil proved
  he could out-stubborn his own namesake. Kaelen has tried, over the years,
  to take over parts of the care routine. Anvil accepts his handling without
  incident. He does not settle for him the way he settles for Vespera.
  Kaelen brings this up more than Anvil seems to care about.
- **Unbothered Under Threat:** True to the species, Anvil doesn't panic.
  Threatened, he withdraws and seals the hatch — a boulder with nothing
  left exposed. This makes him unremarkable on the trail and genuinely
  impossible to press if he decides something is worth sealing up against.

## Relationship with Kaelen & Vespera

Anvil is a fixture of Iron & Oath in the same way the actual anvil is —
load-bearing, permanent, and taken for granted by the couple in the specific
way you take for granted something that has never once let you down in
living memory.

- **Vespera's Charge:** Vespera cleans his shell plates and checks the
  hatch seal at the start and end of every relocation, in the same order
  she's followed for years — the routine itself is the bond, as much as
  affection is. Anvil holds still for this the way he holds still for
  nothing else.
- **Kaelen's Running Joke:** Kaelen refers to Anvil as "the other stubborn
  fixture around here" and has long since stopped expecting the routine to
  transfer to him. He respects the animal's judgment about strangers more
  than he lets on — if Anvil hisses at someone, Kaelen notices.
- **Shared Dependence:** Neither Kaelen nor Vespera says it directly, but
  Iron & Oath's mobility — the fact that it can tear down and rebuild
  somewhere new along the river route every season, with the stock crossing
  the ford unescorted and unharmed every time — depends entirely on Anvil.
  He is not sentimental scenery. He is the stall's supply chain.

## Role in the Story & Gameplay

- **Ambient Tradepost Fixture:** Present at (4, 3) Tradepost alongside Kaelen
  and Vespera. Non-hostile, non-combatant — cannot be drawn into combat.
- **Interaction Flavor:** Responds to `talk`/inspection and `pet` with
  narrated gesture and sound only (no speech — see
  `docs/lore/creatures/shell-back.md` for the species' communication
  profile). Reactions vary depending on whether the narration is framed
  around Vespera, Kaelen, or Anvil alone, reflecting the asymmetry in his
  bond to each of them.
- **World-Building Node:** A concrete, discoverable answer to how a mobile
  forge-and-armor stall manages to relocate — unescorted, across a route
  that defeats ordinary pack animals — along a schedule the nomads actually
  keep. See `docs/lore/creatures/shell-back-history.md` for the full
  domestication history and its tie to the river route's viability as a
  trade corridor.

## Sample Interaction Narration

- *Anvil's sensory stalks swing toward Jean, tracking his hands more than
  his face — an old instinct. He decides Jean isn't reaching for the latch
  and settles back into the scrub at his feet.*
- *(With Vespera nearby)* *She runs a hand along his shell plates in the
  same order she always does, checking the hatch seal without needing to
  look at it. Anvil's foot eases flat against the ground — the specific
  stillness of an animal that recognizes exactly one routine as safe.*
- *(With Kaelen nearby)* *Kaelen raps a knuckle against Anvil's shell on his
  way past. Anvil doesn't withdraw, doesn't react at all — the flat,
  unimpressed tolerance of a very old, very one-sided arrangement.*
- *Jean's hand drifts near the hatch latch and a sharp hiss vents from
  somewhere inside the shell — a clear, specific warning that has nothing
  to do with Jean personally and everything to do with the seal.*
- *Anvil is motionless in a way that reads, at a glance, like a boulder
  someone left at the edge of the stall. Only the faint, steady creak of
  shell plates settling gives him away as something alive.*
