## 3. Findings

### F1 — Container "TAKE ALL" fails on every container, not just the camp crate (corroborates #600)
- **Severity: High** · **Location:** grondia (1,3) Passage, object *Dusty Satchel* (also the generic `take_all` path)
- **Steps:** 1) From the start tile go S to (1,3). 2) INTERACT → 🔍 SEARCH AREA (the satchel is `hidden: true`, `hide_factor: 3`). 3) Click *Dusty Satchel* → OPEN. 4) Click **TAKE ALL**.
- **Expected:** both items move to inventory. **Actual:** red banner `⚠ There's no way for Jean to take_all the Dusty Satchel.` and nothing is taken — `/api/status` gold stayed 315, weight stayed 15.05.
- **Control:** clicking **TAKE** on each row works ("Jean takes Mineral Powder.", "Jean takes Gold."; gold 315→327). The *tile*-level "📦 TAKE ALL ITEMS" in the INTERACT dialog also works ("Jean takes: Mineral Solvent, Padded Cap of Relief, 32× Gold.").
- **Confidence:** reproduced once here, plus the contested camp report; the error string is the engine's generic "no way to <keyword> the <object>", so the container is almost certainly not registering `take_all` as a keyword (engine keywords for this object are `loot/check/examine/open` — no `take_all`).

### F2 — The container dialog's error banner never clears
- **Severity: Low** · **Same tile/dialog as F1**
- After the failed TAKE ALL, the `⚠ There's no way for Jean to take_all…` banner stayed pinned at the top of the dialog through **two subsequent successful TAKEs**, so the screen simultaneously showed an error and "Jean takes Gold." A player reasonably reads that as "the take failed too".

### F3 — Ferry Landing offers two junk keyword buttons
- **Severity: Medium (will-misunderstand)** · **Location:** nomad-camp (0,2) Water Step, object *Ferry Landing*
- The keyword sub-dialog shows three buttons: **ENTER**, **FERRY**, **LANDING**. "FERRY"/"LANDING" are the object's own name split into words presented as actions. (The Eastern Gate shows ENTER + EAST, which reads as a plausible alias; here it is plainly the name.) Expected: one action (ENTER), or clearly-labelled aliases.
- Evidence: dialog buttons `["✕","← BACK","ENTER","FERRY","LANDING"]`.

### F4 — A hidden object is announced in the tile text before it can be interacted with
- **Severity: Low (will-misunderstand)** · **Location:** grondia (1,3)
- The room panel prints *"A stiff, dusty satchel rests in a wall niche."* while `/api/world` reports the satchel `hidden: true`, and INTERACT lists only Gorran. Only after 🔍 SEARCH AREA ("Jean found a forgotten satchel tucked into the wall!") does it appear. The search mechanic is good; the idle line should be withheld (or reworded to a hint) until the object is found, otherwise the interact list looks broken.

### F5 — Gorran contradicts his own scene inside the Mineral Pools
- **Severity: Medium (narrative/state)** · **Location:** grondelith-mineral-pools (2,1) and, after the boss, (2,6)/(2,5)/(2,4)
- Scene 2a states plainly that Gorran "had come to the threshold and no further" and seats him at the arch. One tile in, at Grondelith Atrium (2,1), the room prints the generic **"Gorran is bumbling about."** — the idle line of a wandering NPC, not of someone seated at a threshold he refuses to cross. He is correctly absent from every pools fight. After the King Slime dies he is present again on **every** pools tile including the Arena (2,6), with no beat covering his return.
- Two separate asks: (a) give the waiting Gorran a bespoke idle line; (b) if he is meant to walk in after the cleansing, give it one line of narration.
- Per the primer I checked this against the design intent first — his absence from the *fights* is correct; it is only the tile text and the unexplained re-entry that read wrong.

### F8 — Locked combat moves do not say *why* (pillar 3: every number explainable)
- **Severity: Medium (legibility)** · **Location:** combat move panels, all fights
- Three lock cases, three different levels of help:
  - **Out of range** — good: "Attack ⛔ LOCKED … ⚠ No valid target in range". But the wire already carries `shortfall_ft: 1` ("Rock Rumbler d6, in_range false, shortfall 1") and the UI never shows it. "1 ft out of reach" would turn a dead end into a decision.
  - **On cooldown** — card shows only `Attack ⛔ LOCKED · Fatigue: 88`. The remaining beats live in a *separate* COOLDOWN widget ("⚔ Attack — 5 BEATS"); nothing links the two.
  - **A move already committed** — every other move locks with the engine's literal reason string **"Cannot use this move"**, while `player.move_in_process` says `Turn, beats_until_resolve: 3`. This is the worst of the three: I spent four actions poking a locked Advance before querying the API to learn a queued Turn was blocking it.

### F9 — Combat category buttons render at 42×23 px
- **Severity: Low (polish; would be blocks-play on touch)** · **Location:** combat left column, viewport 951×885
- Measured `getBoundingClientRect()` on OFFENSIVE / MANEUVER / INVENTORY / MISC / DEFENSIVE: **42×23 px each, 11 px label**, because the hero ring is scaled down by a CSS transform as the HEAT / COOLDOWN / ADVISOR / LOG panels stack up (layout `clientWidth` is 76, so this is a transform, not a layout, shrink). The project rule is ≥44 px; these are the five primary combat controls and their labels overflow their boxes.

### F10 — The open move panel sits on top of the category ring and the HP/fatigue bars
- **Severity: Low (polish)** · **Location:** combat, same viewport
- The panel (z-index 100, rect 98,222→418,663) fully covers MISC (179,268→246,305) and DEFENSIVE (275,268→342,305) and part of the HP/fatigue bars, and is translucent enough that those controls bleed through the panel's own title ("MANEUVER MOVES" / "MISCELLANEOUS MOVES" read as double-exposed). **Not** a soft-lock: `document.elementFromPoint` at both button centres returns the panel, so the buried buttons cannot be mis-clicked.

### F11 — Tactical advisor recommended a move for a reason that was false

- Advisor: *"Advance — 80% — Close the distance to bring offensive moves into range"* while the King Slime was at **5 ft**, Attack was available, in range, and flagged `☠ LETHAL` with a 52–78 damage preview. Advancing would have wasted 7 beats under a DEADLY telegraph.

From entering the pools to the arena was ~35 committed actions and roughly 20 minutes of wall-clock at animation speed, for zero risk and one level. Cutting it to two packs, or shortening the Attack cooldown, would lose nothing.

## 5. UX review

Tagged **blocks-play / will-misunderstand / polish**.

1. **Scene pacing is the biggest single UX cost of the beta.** *(will-misunderstand)* Scene 1 alone took ~45 discrete advances; each beat is one or two sentences typed out, and roughly half my Enter presses only completed the typewriter rather than advancing. The Votha Krr audience (Scene 1 + Scene 3) is genuinely good writing, but a first-time player meets ~45 clicks before their first fight. Two cheap wins: let Enter-during-typing complete *and* advance on a second press within the same beat, and let a preference set "text already revealed". Hold-to-skip exists and works, but it skips the whole scene — there is no "faster", only "none".
2. **The next objective is never unclear.** *(strength)* The journal 📖 tracks objectives with ✓ + strike-through and sinks completed ones to the bottom; it ended the run with all 8 ticked. Every hand-off is also stated in dialogue ("The pools are to the southwest", "head to the ferry landing"). The STORY LOG tab (newest-first, tile-headed) let me reconstruct scenes I had clicked past — it is the single most useful piece of UI in the build.
3. **Combat legibility is strong on numbers, weak on *negatives*.** *(will-misunderstand)* Everything you *can* do is explained (accuracy %, damage range, lethal flag, beats, fatigue, heat multiplier, an advisor that quotes the previous cycle). Everything you *cannot* do collapses to "⛔ LOCKED" with either no reason or the string "Cannot use this move" (F8). A player's mental model breaks exactly where the game stops explaining.
4. **No colour-only state anywhere I looked.** *(strength)* ⚔ HOSTILE tags, "⚠ DEADLY", "☠ LETHAL", "⛔ LOCKED", "EQUIPPED", DMG +10 / WT −3 badges — every state carries an icon or word. The battlefield's DEADLY telegraph pairs a red aura with a "⚠13" numeral.
5. **Focus management in dialogs is right.** *(strength)* Focus starts on `.modal-content`, not on the skip button (#584's fix), so Enter/Space advance; story choices document their own shortcut ("Press 1-3 to select") and the number keys work. **Combat move cards have no keyboard path at all** — no numbers, no documented shortcut — which is an odd asymmetry given how many more clicks combat costs than dialogue.
6. **Desktop touch targets fail the project's own 44 px rule in combat** (F9) and the move panel double-exposes the controls behind it (F10). Story/inventory/interact dialogs are fine: the HoldButton is 44 px tall, CONTINUE is 55 px.
7. **Two identically-named "Grondite Elder" NPCs stand at the Citadel** and appear as two identical cards in the INTERACT list with the same description — a player cannot tell which one they already talked to. *(polish)*
8. **Talk with the LLM disabled degrades gracefully.** *(strength)* Mara: "Mara looks at Jean once. Something is calculated, briefly, and filed. She returns to work." Grondite Elder: "The Elder's attention is unhurried and complete… then he is still." One scripted line, no error, no empty panel — a player would read these as characterisation, not as a missing feature.
9. **Loot flow is good but the "skip" is a hair-trigger.** *(polish)* The post-fight COLLECT screen shows current/added/after weight and a "(3 OF 3)" count — genuinely nice. Directly beneath the primary button sits "skip — drop all items on tile →", ~44 px below it; I hit it by accident once and silently dropped the lot. Items survive on the tile, and the INTERACT "📦 TAKE ALL ITEMS" recovered them, so it is not destructive — but the destructive-ish option should not be the nearest neighbour of the primary one.
10. **Prose/voice nits.** Scene 3 congratulates Jean twice in one scene ("The pools are clean, little one. You have done well." then, a few beats later, "The pools are clean. You have done what we could not do alone, little one."). The cleansed pool descriptions are exemplary of the "durable evidence" rule — "long staining, dissolution marks where it ran thickest" — worth holding up as the standard.

## 6. Non-bug observations

- Console across the whole run: **only** the known `403 / [Autosave] Cloud sync failed` pairs (84 of them). No page errors, no failed XHRs other than autosave, no React warnings.
- Start state matched the config except HP: primer says 108, `/api/status` reported **110/110** at level 3.
- The gland rupture events fire per-tile and each opens its own modal *before* the encounter dialog — two CLOSE clicks before you can press FIGHT FOR YOUR LIFE.
- `available_options[].available` is `true` for Attack even when `viable_targets` is empty (out of range); the UI correctly shows LOCKED, but anyone cross-checking over REST will read `available: true` and think the UI is wrong. Worth documenting the contract.
- Attack's fatigue cost drifted 88 → 89 → 75 → 74 across the run as Strength rose; the card updates live, which is good, but nothing tells the player why the number moved.
