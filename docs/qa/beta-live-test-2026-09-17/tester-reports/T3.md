## 4. Findings

### Battlefield drag-to-pan does not move the view at all
- **Severity: High** (confirmed regression of a previously-fixed, QA-relevant feature)
- **Location:** Combat screen, Battlefield Map panel, any fight (tested at Channel Entry, 5-enemy fight)
- **Steps to reproduce:**
  1. Enter any combat with 3+ enemies so some are off-screen ("Enemy off-screen — view widened to Fit Fight" banner appears)
  2. Note the pixel position of the Jean ("J") token via `getBoundingClientRect()`
  3. Perform a mouse drag from the center of the battlefield panel to another point (tested multiple drag vectors, both slow multi-step and fast)
  4. Re-measure the Jean token's bounding box
- **Expected:** The view pans, revealing enemies previously off-screen (per #592's description and the "drag to pan" hint text rendered on the panel)
- **Actual:** Token position is pixel-identical before and after every drag attempt. Tested in both "Fit Fight" and "Overview" view modes — no difference. No console errors accompany the failed drags.
- **Evidence:** Screenshots `T3_006_battlefield_after_drag_pan.png` and `T3_007_battlefield_after_drag_pan2.png` are visually identical to the pre-drag shot `T3_003_battlefield_before_hover.png`. DOM inspection found a `<div style="touch-action:none">` two levels above the "drag to pan" hint text (the correct drag-gesture marker for a gesture library), confirming the intended drag surface was targeted correctly by my mouse events.
- **Confidence:** Reproduced 3 times (2 drag attempts in one fight with different vectors/modes, one measured via precise bounding-box diff)
- **Suspected cause:** Not identified from source (out of scope for this tester to dig into frontend combat code), but the drag handler appears to not be wired to update the camera/pan state, despite the UI still correctly rendering the "drag to pan" affordance text and the gesture-enabling `touch-action: none` CSS.

### React "Maximum update depth exceeded" warning fires in CombatLog on post-victory reload
- **Severity: Low-Medium** (doesn't block the player, but is a real code defect — an actual infinite-update loop warning, not cosmetic)
- **Location:** Combat screen, immediately after `page.reload()` right after a fight's last kill (before Victory dialog interaction)
- **Steps to reproduce:**
  1. Win a fight (last enemy HP hits 0, `battle_state.status` still momentarily "active" before the Victory dialog is dismissed)
  2. `page.reload()` at that moment
  3. Watch the browser console
- **Expected:** No React warnings; UI transitions cleanly to the replayed Victory dialog
- **Actual:** Two `console.error` entries: `"Warning: Maximum update depth exceeded. This can happen when a component calls setState inside useEffect..."` — stack trace points to `CombatLog.jsx:45` rendering under `LeftPanel.jsx:86`. The Victory dialog DID eventually render correctly and loot was collectible, so this is not a hard block, but it indicates `CombatLog` is stuck in a render loop for at least a few cycles during this specific reload timing.
- **Evidence:** Console events seq 76-77 in the driver log; screenshot `T3_011_postfight_reload_stuck.png` shows the Victory dialog that eventually rendered.
- **Confidence:** Seen once (did not attempt to reproduce a second time due to budget)
- **Suspected cause:** `CombatLog.jsx:45` — likely a `useEffect` with a missing/unstable dependency that re-triggers on every render when the combat log array is rebuilt fresh from a post-reload rehydration payload.

- **Severity: Low**

### Stray narration replay when crossing back through the pools passage
- **Severity: Low**
- **Location:** `grondia` (7,9) Grondelith Entrance, immediately after using the "Path to Grondia" passage object to return from the pools

### Inventory reports 25/20 slots used with no apparent consequence
- **Severity: Low / possibly not a bug**
- **Location:** `/api/inventory` response at end of run
- **Observation:** `slots_used: 25` vs `slots_total: 20`, while `weight_percentage: 53.3` (well under the weight cap). No error or blocked pickup was observed at any point despite exceeding the nominal slot count.
- **Confidence:** Seen once, not actively tested (didn't try to pick up an item while at exactly 20 slots to see if a cap is enforced)
- Flagging as "possible intentional mechanic — needs verification" per the CLAUDE.md guidance: weight may be the real constraint and slot count purely informational.

### FIGHT FOR YOUR LIFE sometimes needs a second click after closing a preceding gland dialog
- **Severity: Medium** (looks like a soft-lock to an unaware player)
- **Location:** Any pools tile with both a PulsingGlandEvent and an enemy encounter stacked (observed at Deep West, Deep East, Narrow Pass)
- **Steps to reproduce:**
  1. Move into a tile with a gland event queued before the encounter
  2. Click CLOSE on the gland STORY dialog(s)
  3. Click FIGHT FOR YOUR LIFE as soon as it appears
- **Expected:** Combat UI loads immediately
- **Actual:** On 2 of 5 occasions, the click appeared to do nothing — `battle_state` from `/api/combat/status` showed `active` with full combatant data (so the *engine* had already started combat), but the UI was still showing the pre-combat encounter-announcement dialog with the FIGHT FOR YOUR LIFE button. A second click on the same (seemingly identical) button transitioned to the real combat screen correctly.
- **Evidence:** Reproduced during Deep East and Deep West fights; each time, `get("/api/combat/status")` returned `active` combat state while the UI still showed the announcement dialog until re-clicked.
- **Confidence:** Reproduced twice, seen a 3rd likely occurrence
- **Suspected cause:** A timing race between the gland dialog's close animation and the encounter dialog mounting — the first click may land on a stale/about-to-unmount button reference.

## 7. Non-bug observations

- **Difficulty verdict at level 3 (#581):** the shipped level-3 start is comfortably beatable solo through the entire Mineral Pools arc, including King Slime, *if* the player uses Rest between Attack cooldowns (fatigue gates Attack hard — 96 of a starting 198 fatigue pool per swing). A player who doesn't understand the Rest/fatigue loop could feel stuck ("why can't I attack?") rather than in danger — this is a legibility question more than a balance one. XP gain is fast: two full level-ups (3→4, 4→5) happened organically during ordinary pools trash fights, well before reaching King Slime, so by the time of the boss fight Jean was already over-leveled relative to the "shipped level 3" framing. Whether that's intended pacing (the pools *are* meant to level you up before the boss) or an XP-tuning issue is a judgment call for the maintainer.
- **5-enemy pulls are the norm, not the exception**, in the deeper pools tiles (Channel Entry, North Pocket, Deep East, Narrow Pass, Flooded Pass all spawned 4-5 simultaneous enemies). Combined with Jean fighting alone by design, this makes for a grindy but never actually threatening middle stretch — HP rarely dropped below 80% in any single ordinary fight.
- **Rest/Wait/Attack cycling is friction-heavy in the UI**: each Attack costs ~96 fatigue out of a ~200 pool, so roughly every other action must be a Rest, and Rest itself sometimes needs 2-3 uses to refill enough fatigue for one more swing. This makes ordinary trash fights take noticeably longer in wall-clock/turn count than their difficulty warrants.
- **Level-up mid-loot-collection is a nice touch** — the "Must spend all points to continue expedition" gate before loot collection is clear and the RANDOMIZE button is a good escape hatch for testers/impatient players.
- **Environmental storytelling is strong**: tile descriptions after cleansing ("a dark tide line, scored stone, old acid pitting") match CLAUDE.md's "descriptions are permanent" rule well — durable evidence, not present-tense behavior.
- Autosave-403 toasts, background-music `NotAllowedError`, and CSP Report-Only console lines were seen throughout and correctly ignored as known noise per the primer.
