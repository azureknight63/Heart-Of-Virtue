## 5. Findings

### Finding 1 — Movement controls are hidden behind the MAP bottom tab on mobile (non-obvious first-time UX)
- **Severity**: Low
- **Location**: All exploration screens, mobile viewport (375×812)
- **Steps to reproduce**: 1) Load the game on a 375px-wide viewport. 2) Land on the default CHARACTER tab. 3) Look for a way to move.
- **Expected**: Not specified by the design pillars beyond "mobile-playable" — arguably fine as designed.
- **Actual**: The CHARACTER tab (default landing view) shows only ATTRIBUTES/PARTY/INVENTORY/SKILLS/COMMANDS/INTERACT — no movement affordance at all. Movement (the N/S/E/W/diagonal d-pad) only appears after tapping the separate "🗺️ MAP" bottom tab.
- **Evidence**: `T6_002_t6_00_initial_screen.png` (CHARACTER tab, no move buttons) vs. aria dump after tapping MAP showing `Move North/East/South/West…`.
- **Confidence**: seen once, but structurally guaranteed to recur for any first-time mobile player — this is a design/discoverability question, not a functional bug. Possibly intentional information-architecture choice (keep the description/panel view uncluttered) — flagging per the "ask, don't assume" QA rule rather than filing as a hard bug.
- **Suspected cause**: Deliberate tab split between "Character" and "Map" views in the mobile layout component (not confirmed in source; not looked up given the primer's read-only note about this being layout-related, not logic-related).

### Finding 2 — Intermittent Playwright timeout locating movement buttons immediately after a tab switch (automation-only, unconfirmed as player-facing)
- **Severity**: Low (possibly test-harness artifact, not a game bug)
- **Location**: eastern-descent (0,2)→(1,2) transition, and nomad-camp (3,0)→(3,1) transition
- **Steps to reproduce**: Switch to the MAP tab via touchscreen tap, then immediately attempt to tap a `Move <direction>` button in the same or next scripted step.
- **Expected**: Button locatable within a few hundred ms of tab switch.
- **Actual**: Two occasions where `get_by_role("button", name="move south/east")` timed out at 3000ms right after switching tabs (once right after closing a story dialog, once right after the earlier hold-to-skip interaction). A retry with an explicit fresh tab-switch + wait succeeded immediately.
- **Evidence**: Raw Playwright tracebacks in this session's tool log (`Locator.bounding_box: Timeout 3000ms exceeded … waiting for get_by_role("button", name=re.compile(r"^\s*move south\s*$"...`).
- **Confidence**: seen twice, not reproduced deliberately a third time to isolate; could easily be automation racing the React re-render rather than a real player-facing delay (no real player taps within milliseconds of a tab switch the way a script does).
- **Suspected cause**: Unknown — not investigated in source, flagged only because it recurred.

## 6. Combat log

All three encounters below were **unplanned random encounters** on the eastern-descent map (not story-gated), fought as they triggered along the assigned beeline. Jean is level 3, 110 max HP throughout. Gorran (ally, level 3, 228 max HP) fought alongside Jean in every fight, confirming #577 on this map.

**Fight 1 — Rock Rumbler Rebtoxe** (eastern-descent (1,2) High Ledge, 48 HP)
- Jean 110/110 → 110/110 (no damage taken)
- Gorran took a hit for 23 damage mid-fight
- Jean's Attack (Rusted Iron Mace) landed for 37 damage on the opening exchange; Advance finished it off (engine resolved the kill without a further logged player attack — likely Gorran's follow-up)
- Loot: Polished Rusted Dagger

**Fight 2 — Talus Hound Meserin + Talus Hound Nipiel** (eastern-descent (2,4) Hollow Clearing, 35 HP each)
- Jean 110/110 → 110/110 (no damage taken)
- Gorran took 12 damage from Nipiel
- Jean's Attack hit Meserin for 46 damage (one-shot kill, dropped 41 gold); after an Advance + Wait cycle, a second Attack resolved (UI showed victory before I could log the final hit's damage number) killing Nipiel
- Loot: gold only

**Fight 3 — Talus Hound Danodi + Talus Hound Irfochen** (eastern-descent (3,4) Hounds Warren, 35 HP each)
- Jean 110/110 → 110/110 (no damage taken — Irfochen's one attempted attack on Jean missed)
- Both hounds successfully flanked Jean for +15% damage each while Gorran was mid-Rest — no actual damage landed on Jean from this because their attacks either targeted Gorran or missed
- Jean's fatigue dropped to 2/198 after two Attack+Advance actions, forcing **two separate Rest cycles** (86 FP then 94 FP recovered) before Attack (96 FP cost) was affordable again — Attack briefly showed `⛔ LOCKED … Not enough fatigue`
- Jean's first post-rest Attack **missed** (74% hit chance shown)
- Attack on Danodi hit for 39 damage (one-shot, dropped a Restorative); Attack on Irfochen after the second Rest cycle finished the fight
- Loot: Restorative, Draught, +gold (356 total after)

**Net for the whole run**: Jean took **zero direct HP damage** across 3 fights at level 3 while Gorran (tank/ally) absorbed all incoming hits (ended at 179/228, down 49 HP). This bears directly on the #581 "difficulty at level-3 start" headline question — trash encounters here were essentially risk-free for the player's own HP pool, though the fatigue economy (Attack costs 95-96 FP out of 198 max) forced multiple Rest turns per fight, adding pacing friction without adding danger. Two Talus Hound pairs in a row (Hollow Clearing then Hounds Warren, back-to-back tiles) felt like a lot of trash-mob friction for a ~6-tile beeline; worth a design look at random-encounter density on this stretch.

## 8. Non-bug observations

- Testing hold-to-skip via `page.mouse.down()/up()` (rather than a literal OS-level touch event) worked correctly, consistent with the app listening on pointer events rather than touch-specific events — good for cross-input accessibility, but means the true touchscreen long-press path was not separately isolated.
- The battlefield's tap-to-reveal-HP-numerals feature (#602) is genuinely nicer on mobile than expected: a single tap opens a full stat card (HP bar, stamina bar, status effects, current move), not just a bare numeral — this reads as a deliberate mobile-first design choice and is worth calling out positively.
- Scene 6's gating logic correctly handles the Mara-first traversal order this tester used — no evidence of order-dependence in the `nomad_ferry_ready`-adjacent flag logic based on external behavior (source not inspected).
- The END OF BETA dialog is clean and well-composed on a 375px viewport: centered heading, two clearly differentiated CTA buttons (`CONTINUE EXPLORING` outlined, `SEND FEEDBACK` filled), body text wraps naturally without truncation or overflow.
