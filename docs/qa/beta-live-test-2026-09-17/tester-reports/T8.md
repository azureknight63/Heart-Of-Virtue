## 4. Findings

### Finding 1 — NPC chat first attempt can hang indefinitely with zero UI feedback
- **Severity:** Critical
- **Location:** nomad-camp (3,2) Fire Ring, Devet's chat dialog
- **Steps to reproduce:** 1) Reach Devet at Fire Ring. 2) INTERACT → Devet → talk. 3) Observe dialog state.
- **Expected:** Either a generated reply, or (if degraded) an honest fallback banner within a bounded time.
- **Actual:** Dialog opened showing "Waiting for Devet…" and stayed there for **over 90 seconds** with no change. `View History` reported "0 turns on record — Nothing has been said yet." throughout. `End Conversation` stayed **disabled** the entire time — the only way out was the generic `✕` close button (a hard abandon, not a graceful end). No console error, no failed/4xx/5xx network event was ever logged during the wait (`allerrors` empty for this window).
- **Evidence:** Screenshot `logs/qa/runs-0917/shots/T8_001_devet_hang.png`. `allerrors` output attached above shows zero chat-related entries despite the multi-wait window.
- **Confidence:** Seen once at this severity (≥90s, gave up); a related but shorter hang (~40s before recovering to fallback) reproduced on the 3rd Devet attempt, and the exact same "stuck on gathering/waiting with no timeout" pattern reproduced a **second time** on Devet's 2nd conversation turn (60s+, never resolved — see Finding 2). So the *pattern* (no client-side timeout, no error surfaced) reproduced 3/3 times on Devet; only the wait duration varied.
- **Suspected cause:** Frontend chat panel (`src/components/NpcChatPanel.jsx` / `src/hooks/useNpcChat.js` per the loaded JS chunk) appears to have no client-side request timeout and no polling/backoff indicator; if the backend LLM call stalls (e.g., provider retry/rotation taking long per `.claude/rules/llm-prompts.md`'s fallback-chain notes), the player is left on "Waiting for..." with no way to know if it's still working or dead, and cannot cleanly end the conversation until it resolves.

### Finding 2 — Zero real (non-fallback) LLM output obtained for Devet in 3 attempts; fallback ignores the chosen reply option
- **Severity:** High
- **Location:** Devet chat (Fire Ring)
- **Steps to reproduce:** Talk to Devet 3 times (1 initial + 1 retry + 1 fresh reopen); on the one turn that got past the opener, pick any dialogue option.
- **Expected:** A live-generated reply appropriate to Jean's chosen option (per T5's fix — model auto-discovery restart).
- **Actual:** All 3 Devet attempts either hung (Finding 1) or resolved to the honest degraded banner "😐 neutral · Neutral — Connection unavailable — this reply is scripted, not generated." followed by one of Devet's own `conversation_starters_by_chapter["1"]` lines verbatim (not a real reply to context). On the one 2nd-turn attempt I made (after Jean picked "Tell me more."), the "reply" shown was **not connected to the question** — it was just another static starter, unrelated to "tell me more." This directly answers the assignment's UI-degradation question: the banner is honest about being scripted, but the scripted content doesn't attempt to answer what the player asked.
- **Evidence:** Screenshot `T8_002_devet_fallback_banner.png`. Contrast with Mara (Finding 4) who got 3/3 real generated replies on the same stack in the same session — suggesting per-NPC/session flakiness rather than a global outage.
- **Confidence:** Reproduced 3/3 for Devet.

### Finding 3 — Conversation ends silently with no closing line, no closing screen
- **Severity:** High
- **Location:** Liss chat (Camp Far Edge) and Mara chat (Rivers Edge)
- **Steps to reproduce:** 1) Talk to Liss, exchange 2 turns. 2) Observe dialog after picking the 2nd option. Repeated with Mara after 4 turns.
- **Expected:** Per `ai/npc/human/liss.json` / `mara.json`, each NPC has explicit `closing_lines_when_exhausted` (e.g. Mara: "Mara turns back to what she was doing. The conversation is over."; Liss: "Liss loses interest abruptly and goes back to looking at the stone.") meant to signal the conversation is over.
- **Actual:** In both cases the chat dialog reverted directly and silently to the plain object-interact dialog (`✨ Liss` / `✨ Mara` showing only description + `talk` button again) — no closing line, no toast, no "conversation ended" state, nothing in `allerrors`. The player has no way to tell whether the conversation is truly over or whether something broke.
- **Evidence:** Screenshot `T8_004_liss_dialog_closed.png`.
- **Confidence:** Reproduced 2/2 (Liss after her 2nd turn, Mara after her 4th turn) — both NPCs whose loquacity budget appears to have been hit, both silent.
- **Suspected cause:** The `closing_lines_when_exhausted` array in the NPC JSON is defined but (on this evidence) never rendered by the frontend chat panel when the backend signals exhaustion — it looks like the dialog is simply torn down instead of showing a final beat.

### Finding 4 — Mara's live chat is the one clean success: 3 real generated exchanges, correct fallback banner absent

- **Location:** Mara chat (Rivers Edge)

The 4th turn produced no output before the dialog silently closed (Finding 3), so it's unclear whether a 4th reply was ever attempted or the conversation was already at its loquacity cap.

### Finding 5 — Low: player-side dialogue-option labels reuse strings from the NPC's own prohibited-phrase list
- **Severity:** Low
- **Location:** Liss chat, turn 2 options
- **Detail:** The offered player-reply buttons included `"Fair enough. [direct]"` and `"I see. [guarded]"`. Both `"fair enough"` and `"I see"` are in `liss.json`'s `prohibited_phrases`. These are Jean's lines, not Liss's, so this is not a contract violation as written (the prohibition is scoped to the NPC's own generated voice), but since both sides of the exchange appear to come from the same generation pipeline, it's worth a second look in case the same phrase-ban should extend to player-option phrasing for tonal consistency.
- **Confidence:** Seen once; possibly intentional (player options are deliberately more "modern/neutral" in register than the NPC's voice).

## 7. Non-bug observations

- Random roaming encounters (Rock Rumbler, 2× Talus Hound pairs) are frequent enough on the short eastern-descent route (3 encounters in ~10 tiles) that they ate the majority of this session's tool-call budget before reaching the camp — worth knowing for future taxed-budget QA runs of this route.
- The `😐 neutral` / `😐 curious` emotion-portrait tag rendered correctly and consistently for every NPC/turn.
- The three canned dialogue-option buttons (direct/guarded/open) are well-written and NPC-appropriate even when the underlying reply degrades — Mara's and Liss's option phrasing stayed in-voice for the *player's* line.
- Devet and Liss's `conversation_starters_by_chapter` lines are strong, evocative prose on their own — the issue isn't the writing, it's that live generation never actually fired for either of them this session, so the "voice" being graded was actually just pre-written text serving as a safety net.
