## 3. Balance — the headline

### The damage line, so the numbers are interpretable

`src/moves/_base.py::resolve_damage` computes
`(((faced_power * resistance) - protection) * heat) * variance`, i.e. **protection is subtracted
after the resistance multiplier**. Against a Corrupted Stone Creature (protection 18, crushing 1.6,
slashing 0.4) that turns the weapon choice into a cliff, not a slope: the mace multiplies *before*
the subtraction, the shortsword divides before it.

### Corrupted Stone Creature — engine truth (read from `/api/combat/status`)

```
hp 60/60  |  defense(protection) 18  |  attack_power 22  |  accuracy 91  |  evasion 5  |  speed 5
resistances: crushing 1.6, fire 1.4, earth 0.5, piercing 0.4, slashing 0.4  (all others 1.0)
```

### Fight A — (2,2), **Rusted Iron Mace**, **level 4**

| Beat | Move | Weapon | UI-advertised range | Rolled | Enemy HP before → after | Jean took |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1→7 | Advance (on Stone Creature) | — | — | — | 60 → 60 | 0 |
| 7→16 | **Attack** | Rusted Iron Mace | *(none shown — see #555)* API preview 64–97 | **81** | **60 → 0 (killed)** | 1 |

**One hit. Nine beats. One HP lost.** Loot: Mineral Solvent.

The same encounter also spawned 4 random Slimes (20 HP, 26 attack power). Killing all four with the
mace took a further ~70 beats and 7 more HP; damage rolls 91 / 78 / 78 / 85 (Crusader's Oath active).
Encounter total: 5 enemies, ~85 beats, **8 HP lost of 100**.

### Fight B — (2,4), **Shortsword**, **level 5** (stronger Jean than Fight A)

| Beat | Move | Weapon | API preview | Rolled | Enemy HP | Jean took |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1→7 | Advance | — | — | — | 60 | 0 |
| 15 | Attack | Shortsword | **min 3 / max 4, `lethal: false`** | 3 | 60 → 57 | 0 |
| 30 | Attack | Shortsword | 3–4 | 3 | 57 → 54 | 0 |
| 45 | Attack | Shortsword | 3–4 | 4 | 54 → 50 | 0 |
| … | ×10 more attacks, interleaved with Rest every other action | | | 5,4,5,4,4,5,5,4,6,6 | | 2,3,4,2,1,5,4 |
| ~255 | killing blow | Shortsword | | | **60 → 0** | |

Recorded hits on the Stone Creature: **13 logged for 58 damage** (log is scroll-capped; the killing
blow is the 14th). Damage crept from 3 → 6 only because **heat** rose over the long fight.

### The comparison the maintainer asked for

| | Mace (lvl 4) | Shortsword (lvl 5) | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Damage per hit vs Stone Creature | **81** | **3–6** | ~18× |
| Hits to kill | **1** | **~14** | 14× |
| Beats elapsed | **9** | **~248** | 27× |
| Jean HP lost | **1** | **26** | 26× |
| Player actions (attack + forced Rest) | 1 | ~27 | 27× |

**The wrong-weapon choice is not "punishing", it is a different game.** It does not make the fight
unwinnable at level 4–5 — Jean out-heals it trivially, the slimes only chip 1–5 HP a hit — but it
turns a nine-beat exchange into a four-minute rest-loop with no feedback explaining why. My honest
read: the *ratio* is fine as a design statement; what is broken is that **nothing in the UI tells the
player which side of it they are on**, and one widget actively points them at the wrong side
(Finding 3).

### Winnable / losable / escapable

- **Winnable:** yes, comfortably, at level 4 with the mace. Never dropped below 92/100 in the first
  encounter.
- **Losable:** not realistically at level 4+. Slimes and cave bats produced 21 "struck Jean but did no
  damage" lines against leather armour (protection 31 by level 5). Jean's low-water mark for the whole
  run was 60/130, reached by *choosing* to fight a Stone Creature with the wrong weapon.
- **Escapable: NO.** `available_options` in every encounter contained exactly:
  `Check, Wait, Rest, Turn, Use Item, Advance, Withdraw, Attack, Shoot Bow, Shoot Crossbow, Dodge,
  Crusader's Oath`. **There is no Flee/Retreat/Disengage move.** `Withdraw` only repositions inside
  the arena; the fight continues. Once engaged you must win.
- **#543 (move refused mid-combat): VERIFIED FIXED** —
  `post("/api/world/move", {"direction":"north"})` during the (2,2) fight returned
  `{'error': 'Cannot move while in combat', 'success': False}`.

### King Slime — is it a satisfying boss at this level?

```
hp 400/400 | attack_power 50 | accuracy 90 | defense 15 | evasion 4 | speed 6
resistances: fire 1.5, crushing 1.2, earth 0.9, piercing 0.65, slashing 0.65
```

Jean: level 5, 128/130 HP, Rusted Iron Mace, no buffs. API attack preview: **52–78, `lethal: false`,
hit 91%.**

| # | Action | Beat | Jean HP | King Slime HP | Damage dealt | Damage taken |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Attack | 1→10 | 128 → 128 | 400 → 340 | **60** | 0 |
| 2 | Rest | 10→17 | 128 | 340 | — | 0 |
| 3 | Attack | 17→26 | 128 → 112 | 340 → 265 | **75** | 16 |
| 4 | Rest | 26→33 | 112 | 265 | — | 0 |
| 5 | Attack | 33→42 | 112 | 265 → 201 | **64** | 0 |
| 6 | Rest | 42→49 | 112 → 99 | 201 | — | 13 |
| 7 | Attack | 49→58 | 99 | 201 → 129 | **72** | 0 |
| 8 | Rest | 58→65 | 99 | 129 | — | 0 |
| 9 | Attack | 65→74 | 99 | 129 → 62 | **67** | 0 |
| 10 | Rest | 74→81 | 99 | 62 | — | 0 (KS missed) |
| 11 | Attack | 81→86 | 99 | **62 → 0** | **88** | 0 |

**Verdict: not a satisfying boss at level 5.** Numbers:

- **6 attacks, 5 Rests, 86 beats.** Every mace roll (60–88) landed inside the advertised 52–78 band
  except the last (88, heat-inflated).
- Jean lost **29 of 130 HP (22%)** and never fell below **76%**. He was never in danger; no
  Restorative was needed, no defensive move was ever worth using.
- **Move variety: zero.** The King Slime used exactly one offensive line all fight —
  *"King Slime lashes out at Jean with extreme violence!"* — three times, landing twice (16 and 13
  damage) and missing once. Its other logged actions were `rests for a moment` ×4 and one flavour
  line. A 400 HP boss with a single 50-power basic attack, defense 15 and speed 6 is a damage sponge,
  not an encounter. It never used a slam, a split, an adds phase, a status effect, or a positional
  threat.
- **Fatigue, not HP, is the only real constraint.** Attack costs 65–69 of ~220 max fatigue with a
  4-beat cooldown, so the entire fight is a forced `Attack → Rest → Attack → Rest` metronome. That is
  the whole tactical content of the boss.

If the intent is that a level 3–4 player arrives here, the pressure has to come from somewhere other
than the King Slime's damage output — a second phase, adds, or an attack that punishes standing
adjacent through the recoil window.

## 4. Findings

### Finding 1 — Post-cleanse pool text renders as an object literally named `null`, and the corrupted tile description never changes

- **Severity: High** (player-visible raw internals + directly contradictory prose; also a #553-family
  leak on the exact tiles #553 was about)
- **Location:** `grondelith-mineral-pools` (2,2), (2,3), (2,4), (2,5), (2,6) — every pool tile checked
  after the King Slime died
- **Steps to reproduce:**
  1. Reach `(2,6) GrondelithArena` and defeat the King Slime.
  2. Clear the victory / loot / memory-flash dialogs.
  3. Read the room panel on (2,6), then walk north through (2,5), (2,4), (2,3), (2,2).
- **Expected:** the tile description is replaced by the cleansed version.
- **Actual:** the corrupted description is still printed in full, and the cleansed text is appended
  **below it as an object line prefixed with the literal string `null`**. `where()` reports
  `objects: ["null"]` on every one of those tiles. The two texts flatly contradict each other on the
  same screen.
- **Evidence** — (2,5), verbatim from `text()`:

  ```
  GRONDELITH APPROACH
  The cavern narrows again. The rumbling is a physical presence now ... The green slime on the
  walls shudders with each pulse, as if breathing. The passage south opens into a vast space
  full of pulsing verdant light.
  null The passage is open and still. The walls are bare stone — stained where the slime reached,
  but bare. The rumbling is gone. ...
  ```

  (2,4): `null The passage is as narrow as ever … but the slime that coated them is gone …` while the
  paragraph above still says `the slime coating them making passage distinctly unpleasant`.
  (2,6): `null The circular cavern is still. The pool that filled it … is gone.` while the paragraph
  above still says `An immense pool of pulsating green slime fills the chamber from wall to wall`.

  `where()` at (2,6): `{"objects": ["null"], "items": ["Epee"], "npcs": ["Gorran"]}`
  Screenshots: `T5_039_T5-41-post-boss.png`, `T5_042_T5-45-null-object-cleanse-text.png`
- **Confidence:** reproduced on **five** tiles in one pass. Not intentional — no design would print
  the string `null`.

  2. Read the `VS. EQUIPPED` block.
  3. Equip the Shortsword, then open the Rusted Iron Mace and read the same block.

### Finding 5 — #557 is not fully fixed: a tall move panel covers the whole category tab row

- **Severity: High** (UI action not working with a real pointer; recoverable only by closing the panel)
- **Location:** combat screen, desktop 1280×900
- **Steps to reproduce:**
  1. Enter any combat.
  2. Open the **MISC** category (5 entries: Check / Wait / Rest / Shoot Bow / Crusader's Oath).
  3. Try to click **DEFENSIVE** (or any other category tab).

### Finding 6 — The server computes a per-target damage preview and the UI never renders it

- **Severity: High** (this is the substance of #555; the number the player needs exists and is discarded)
- **Location:** combat — move cards and the 🎯 SELECT TARGET dialog
- **Steps to reproduce:**
  1. In combat with a target in range, open OFFENSIVE and read the Attack card.
  2. Click Attack and read the target picker.
  3. Compare with `GET /api/combat/status` → `available_options[Attack].target_previews`.
- **Expected:** the advertised range predicts the roll (and is advertised).
- **Actual:** the API ships, per target,
  `{"damage_preview": {"min": 64, "max": 97, "lethal": true}, "hit_chance": 87, "is_ally": false}`.
  The Attack card's full text is `Attack / Fatigue: 69 / 9 beats / Strike at your enemy with your
  equipped weapon.` — no damage. The target picker shows only `HP: 60/60` and `Accuracy: 87%`.
  `grep -rn "damage_preview" frontend/src` returns **zero hits** — the field is never read anywhere in
  the frontend.
- **The predictions are good.** Every roll I measured fell in band: Stone Creature/mace preview 64–97
  → rolled 81; Stone Creature/shortsword preview 3–4 → rolled 3, 3, 4 (later 5–6 as heat rose);
  King Slime preview 52–78 → rolled 60, 75, 64, 72, 67 (and 88 at heat 1.60).
  So the fix went the wrong way: the misleading range was removed rather than corrected, leaving a
  correct one unused. The `lethal` flag is the single most useful thing here — it would have told a
  shortsword player instantly that they cannot win the exchange.
- **Also unexplained:** `"Slime Ondexerawwas struck Jean but did no damage!"` still appears with no
  explanation (21 occurrences in one fight). There is no glossary entry reachable at that moment; the
  `?` "Open combat glossary" button exists but nothing in the log links a zero-damage line to
  protection.
- **Confidence:** reproduced across four fights plus a source grep.

### Finding 11 — `max_fatigue` disagrees between the exploration panel and combat, and debug-set attributes don't refresh `maxhp` until a fight

- **Actual:** immediately after the level-4 boost, `/api/debug/player` reported
  `maxhp 100, maxfatigue 190`, and the room panel showed `100/100` and `190/190`. On entering combat,
  `battle_state.player.max_fatigue` was **213** and, after the fight, `where().maxhp` was **126**.
  Derived stats are evidently recomputed lazily on combat entry rather than when attributes change.
  Only reachable via the debug routes, so it is a QA-harness wart rather than a player-facing bug —
  but it means **a boosted tester's HP bar under-reports until the first fight**. Flagging so the next
  boosted run doesn't mistake it for a balance observation.
- **Confidence:** reproduced.

## 7. Non-bug observations

- **Fatigue is the only pacing mechanism in combat.** `Attack` costs 65–69 of ~220 max fatigue with a
  4-beat cooldown, so every fight becomes `Attack → Rest → Attack → Rest`. It works, but it means
  Dodge, Turn, Withdraw and Crusader's Oath are never worth a beat at this level.
- **The Tactical Advisor runs with the LLM off.** It printed heuristic suggestions
  (`▲ Advance 80% — Close the distance to bring offensive moves into range`) and an
  `ANALYSIS OF PREVIOUS CYCLE` block quoting the log. Useful, and it costs nothing — but it also sat
  on `ANALYZING COMBAT SITUATION…` for long stretches, which reads like a hang.
- **Encounter density in the pools is high.** (2,2) spawned the authored Stone Creature **plus four**
  random slimes; (2,3) spawned five; (2,4) five. Each spawn opens its own `✨ STORY` dialog that has to
  be dismissed one at a time before the combat UI appears — five modal dismissals before the player
  can act. Batching them into one panel would save a lot of clicking.
- **Loot flow is good.** The `SPOILS OF BATTLE` screen showing carry weight before/after
  (`16.8 → 17.3 / 44.0 lb`) with per-item checkboxes is genuinely nice.
- **Journal objectives track correctly**, including the beta-briefing checklist — the King Slime
  objective flipped to ✓ immediately on the kill.
- **Autosave 403 spam**: three console errors + one failed POST every ~5 s for the whole session.
  Known noise per the primer, but at ~2 500 events over a 50-minute run it makes `allerrors` genuinely
  hard to read. A single suppressed-after-first-failure toast would help testers.
