### Debug intervention (disclosed)

Jean died twice playing honestly (F1 at the first Mineral Pools fight, F7 to the King Slime).
Test sessions cannot save and `START OVER` returns to the login page (F9), so each death cost the
whole run. On the third driver session I ran, **once**, before replaying:

```
post("/api/debug/player/level",      {"level": 3, "exp": 370})
post("/api/debug/player/attributes", {"attributes": {"strength": 17, "endurance": 15, "speed": 15}})
post("/api/debug/player/restore",    {})
```

Those are exactly the level and stats Jean had **already earned by hand** in run 2 at the same point
in the route (verified against the run-2 `/api/debug/player` dump). This is a restore, not the
level-4 boost. Everything after that — every fight, every level-up, every point allocation — was
played normally. Levels 4 and 5 were earned in-run. **All level-1 difficulty judgements in this
report come from runs 1 and 2, before any debug call.**

## Findings

### F1 — Critical/High: the shipped level-1 start dies in the first authored Mineral Pools fight

- **Severity:** High (a first-time beta player loses their run at the first authored encounter; not an
  absolute blocker, because it *is* winnable at level 1 with correct play — see below)
- **Location:** `grondelith-mineral-pools` **(2, 2) Grondelith Channel Entry**
- **Repro (from session start, shipped config, no boost):**
  1. Clear `BetaTesterBriefing`, walk (1,2)→(7,5), play `Ch02GuideToCitadel` (lands at Citadel (10,5)).
  2. Walk (10,5)→(7,9), interact `Passage to the Mineral Pools` → enter.
  3. Move south ×2 to (2,2). Authored spawner fires: **4 Slimes (20 HP each) + 1 Corrupted Stone
     Creature (60 HP)**, all hostile, Jean solo at level 1 with 100 HP.
- **Expected:** a level-1 opening encounter the player can lose only through bad play.
- **Actual (run 1, no potions used):** dead at beat 106.
  `[13:41:22] Slime Lokahisechban struck Jean for 20 damage! / You have been defeated!`
  Jean killed the Stone Creature and **not one slime** in 106 beats.
- **Why it is that tight, with numbers:**
  - `Attack` costs **113 fatigue** out of a level-1 pool of **152** and has an 11–13 beat cooldown.
    `Rest` restores 52–70 FP. So the loop is **attack → rest → rest → attack**: Jean acts offensively
    once per ~3 exchanges while **five** enemies act every exchange.
  - `allies: []` in every single fight — **Gorran does not join** (see F8). The party of two is a
    party of one in combat.
  - Slime hits landed for 1–20; the killing blow was 20 on a 100 HP bar.
- **It is winnable at level 1** — run 2 beat it in 26 rounds using Restoratives and targeting slimes
  (the mace one-shots a 20 HP slime), but Jean bottomed out at **6/100 HP** and had to drink.
- **Everything after this is easy.** The fight awards enough exp to reach level 2, (2,3) reaches
  level 3, (2,4) reaches level 4-5. The (2,4) authored fight — identical composition — cost me
  **zero HP**. The difficulty curve is a cliff followed by a flat.
- **Evidence:** `T1_027` (5 enemies at open), `T1_033` (46-damage mace hit), `T1_036` (DEFEAT modal),
  `T1_035`/`T1_041` (run-2 win).
- **Confidence:** reproduced across two independent level-1 sessions (one loss, one win).

### F2 — High: `GorranGestureEvent` can never fire; the Grondia farewell scene is unreachable

- **Severity:** High (authored story scene never plays)
- **Location:** `eastern-descent` **(0, 2) Grondia Eastern Gate**
- **Repro:**
  1. Walk Grondia (14,5)→(15,5) Gate East directionally.
  2. INTERACT → `Eastern Gate` → `enter`. You land on `eastern-descent (0,2)`.
  3. `pending: []`, no dialog. Walk east to (1,2), then west back onto (0,2): still `pending: []`.
- **Expected:** the Gorran farewell scene, per `src/story/ch03.py:45-79`.
- **Actual:** it never runs.
- **Root cause (read from source + map JSON):** the event is placed on `eastern-descent (0,2)`
  (`eastern-descent.json`: `"(0,2)" … "GorranGestureEvent"`). Its `check_conditions` requires
  `previous_tile` to be a Grondia tile (#547 guard). But **the only way into (0,2) from Grondia is
  the `Eastern Gate` object teleport** — Grondia (15,5) has `exits: {west}` only — and arriving by
  teleport does not run the destination tile's events. The only *directional* arrival onto (0,2) is
  walking west from (1,2), at which point `previous_tile` is an eastern-descent tile and the #547
  guard correctly rejects it. The two conditions are mutually exclusive.
- **Evidence:** `T1_019` (arrival, no event), `T1_021` (walk-west arrival, no event); tile/event
  placement quoted from `src/resources/maps/eastern-descent.json`.
- **Confidence:** reproduced twice (both arrival paths) plus source confirmation.

### F3 — High: after the King Slime, the arena spawns an object literally named `null`, and the tile description still describes the living boss

- **Severity:** High (player-visible broken string; and a permanent-description violation of the
  design pillar "descriptions must stay true after NPCs die")
- **Location:** `grondelith-mineral-pools` **(2, 6) Grondelith Arena**, after `AfterDefeatingKingSlime`
- **Repro:** kill the King Slime, clear the memory flash, read the room panel / open INTERACT.
- **Expected:** the arena tile's own description is replaced with the cleansed text.
- **Actual, two problems in one:**
  1. `where()` reports `objects: ["null"]`. The INTERACT panel lists an object whose **name is the
     four-character string `null`**, with the cleansed-arena prose as its description:
     `🪵 null — The circular cavern is still. The pool that filled it … is gone.`
     The room panel prints it inline: `null The circular cavern is still. …`
  2. The tile description itself is **unchanged**: *"An immense pool of pulsating green slime fills
     the chamber from wall to wall … Something colossal shifts beneath the surface — a mountain of
     corrupted mass, slowly rising."* — describing a boss that is dead and a pool that is clean, on
     the same screen as the text saying both are gone.
- **Evidence:** `T1_070` (room panel), `T1_072` (INTERACT panel a11y tree quoted above).
- **Confidence:** reproduced twice (persisted across the memory-flash scene and a later revisit).

### F4 — High: "Hold to skip the rest of this scene" does nothing

- **Severity:** High (advertised control is completely inert; combined with the 93-stage
  `Ch02GuideToCitadel` this is real friction for a beta tester replaying the route)
- **Location:** every story dialog; measured on `BetaTesterBriefing`, Grondia (1,2)
- **Repro:** advance to stage 2 (button reports `is_disabled() == False`), then hold it.
- **Expected:** a hold-to-confirm fill, then the scene ends.
- **Actual, three attempts, all negative:**
  1. `page.mouse.down()` on the button centre for 3 s → nothing.
  2. Held for **8 s**, sampling the label every second — label never changes, no progress indicator

  3. Synthetic `pointerdown` + `mousedown`, 4 s, then `pointerup`/`mouseup` → nothing.
  `hit_test` shows the button reachable (`stack_has_element: True`, top node is its own `SPAN`).
- **Evidence:** `T1_002_t1-32-holdskip-long.png`.
- **Confidence:** reproduced three ways in one sitting.

### F5 — High: the conversation stage never takes keyboard focus, so "Click, Space or Enter to continue" is a lie until you mouse-click

- **Severity:** High (a keyboard-only player is stuck at the first VN stage of every scene; the
  on-screen instruction is wrong)
- **Location:** every VN-mode story stage (first hit at Grondia (7,5), `Ch02GuideToCitadel`)
- **Repro:**
  1. Reach a stage showing `▾ Click, Space or Enter to continue`.
  2. Press Enter. Nothing. I pressed it **39 more times**; the stage never advanced.
     `document.activeElement` was `BUTTON` (the CONTINUE button from the previous stage), not the
     stage.
  3. Click anywhere in the dialog body → it advances, and `activeElement` becomes
     `DIV.conversation-stage conversation-stage--default`.
  4. Space and Enter now both work — **until the next stage that has a CONTINUE button**. Clicking
     that button moves focus back to `BUTTON`, and Enter is dead again. I confirmed this by pressing
     Enter (no change) then clicking (change) with `activeElement` logged before and after.
- **Expected:** the stage takes focus when it opens and re-takes it after a button-driven stage, or
  the key handler is bound at document level.
- **Evidence:** `T1_017`; `focused before: BUTTON:` / `enter changed: False` / `click changed: True`
  / `focused after click: DIV:conversation-stage …`.
- **Confidence:** reproduced twice with explicit `activeElement` logging.

### F6 — High: a z-index 2100 overlay covers the object-interaction dialog after a keyword narration; only Escape frees it

- **Severity:** High (UI action not working — a mouse-only player cannot close the dialog or press a
  second keyword; not a hard soft-lock, because Escape works)
- **Location:** object dialogs. Seen on Grondia (1,2) `Scratched Tally` and eastern-descent (3,2)
  `Carved Stone Marker`.
- **Repro:** INTERACT → an object → press a keyword (`read`) → wait for the narration → try to click
  `← Back`, `✕`, or the other keyword.
- **Expected:** the dialog's own controls stay clickable.
- **Actual:** Playwright reports the click intercepted. `hit_test` on `✕`:
  `stack: ['DIV|z=2100|pe=auto', 'BUTTON|z=auto|pe=auto', 'DIV|z=auto|pe=auto',
  'DIV.modal-content |z=auto|pe=auto', 'DIV.modal-overlay |z=2000|pe=auto', …]`,
  `top_is_element: False`. A `DIV` at **z 2100** sits above the object dialog's own overlay (z 2000)
  and eats every pointer event in the dialog. On the Scratched Tally the interceptor was reported by
  name: `<div>Jean begins reading...↵Three grouped scratches, t…</div> … intercepts pointer events`.
  `raw_click` returns `True` but the keyword still produced no new text. **`Escape` closes it and
  restores the game.**
- **Evidence:** `T1_008`, `T1_025_t1-87-hittest.png`, hit_test output above.
- **Confidence:** reproduced on two different objects on two different maps.

### F7 — High: the King Slime "surge" hits for 84, which can exceed the whole HP bar at the intended level, and the only warning is one line in a scrolling log

- **Severity:** High (fairness pillar: "warn before lethal danger". A warning exists, but it is not
  presented as a warning.)
- **Location:** `grondelith-mineral-pools` **(2, 6) Grondelith Arena**
- **Repro:** fight the King Slime at level 3 (which is what my honest run-2 play produced by the time
  I arrived — 114 max HP).
- **Actual, verbatim from the combat log:**
  ```
  [14:06:43] King Slime's entire mass draws inward — the pool around it recedes with a terrifying
             suction. The arena floor shudders. It is about to surge.
  [14:06:50] King Slime erupts — a solid wall of corrupted mass crashes across the stone and slams
             into Jean!
  [14:06:50] King Slime struck Jean for 84 damage!
  [14:06:50] Jean is coated in corrosive slime!
  [14:06:50] You have been defeated!
  ```
  Jean was at 79/114 — above two thirds — and died in one blow. Every other King Slime hit that
  fight was 0–18.
- **The telegraph is correctly authored** and is one beat ahead. But it is rendered as an ordinary
  grey line in the auto-scrolling combat log, with no colour, no banner, no battlefield marker and no
  change to the Tactical Advisor's suggestion (which still said "Attack"). Nothing tells the player
  this specific line means *"heal or dodge now or die."*
- **Suggested fix (design call, not filed as a defect):** surface the telegraph the way the
  off-screen-enemy banner is surfaced (F-verify #561 shows the pattern already exists), and/or cap
  the surge at a fraction of max HP.
- **On a level-5 replay the King Slime never landed a single hit** (speed 23), so the encounter
  swings from one-shot-lethal to trivial across two levels.
- **Evidence:** `T1_029_t1-61-kingslime-death.png` (full log), `T1_028`.
- **Confidence:** seen once, but the log is unambiguous and the numbers are reproducible from it.

## Dialogue voice notes

No LLM was live on the `full` stack, so everything below is scripted prose. No `prohibited_phrases`
check applies (nothing was generated).

- **Votha Krr** — held character well. Economical, faintly amused, never explains himself:
  *"To mend what is broken, one must first understand the cracks."* The beat where he takes the
  fragment and simply **puts it in his mouth**, then answers a question by pressing two fingers to
  his chest and withdrawing, is the best writing I saw on the route.
- **Jean** — the interiority lands. Two moments stood out: the half-lifted hand toward his brow in
  the Citadel nave ("He let the old gesture finish. It steadied him."), and the convection
  observation, which characterises him as a builder without saying so.
- **`Ch02KingSlimeMemoryFlash`** — the strongest scene in the run. The cut finger as the anchor, and
  *"And where something warm should have been, in Jean's arms — emptiness."* No text truncation, no
  blank stages, portraits correct throughout.
- **Devet** — dry, unimpressed, consistent. *"Long enough I don't remember what I was doing before."*
- **Mara** — sharp and funny without breaking tone: *"That's either agreement or he's warming up to
  eat something. How can you tell the difference?"* The detail of her noticing Jean notice the
  smooth-handled object, and filing both away without comment, is good craft.
- **Gorran** — carried entirely by gesture and rumble, as designed. Never speaks. Consistent.

## Non-bug observations

- **Combat is slow in wall-clock terms.** A 5-enemy pool fight at level 1 is ~26 rounds and ~7 real
  minutes, most of it spent resting because `Attack` costs 113 of 152 fatigue. It is legible and
  tactical, but a beta tester will feel the length before they feel the tactics.
- **The Victory → level-up → loot chain is a three-modal gauntlet** and each modal appears on a
  delay, so the movement panel is unavailable for several seconds after a fight with no indication
  that something is still loading. I mistook it for a stuck screen four separate times.
- **`Ch02GuideToCitadel` is ~93 stages long** and fires on the mandatory (7,5) tile with no working
  skip (F4). Anyone replaying the route sits through all of it.
- The **out-of-combat INVENTORY panel opens on the WEAPONS tab**, so healing takes an extra click
  through `CONSUMABLES`; the in-combat one opens on Consumables. Small inconsistency, felt every time.
- The `null` object aside (F3), the **cleanse payoff is well staged** — walking back up a corridor of
  now-empty tiles after the boss is a genuinely good beat, and no enemies respawn on the way out.
- **Encounter density in the pools is high**: four of the six tiles on the (2,y) column spawn a fight,
  three of them with five enemies. At level 1 that is a wall; by (2,5) it is filler.
