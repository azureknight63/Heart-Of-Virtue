## 3. Findings

### F1 — Conversation scenes cannot be advanced by keyboard; the on-screen hint says they can (#530 regression)

- **Severity: High** (UI action not working; keyboard operability)
- **Location:** any conversation-style story scene. Reproduced at Grondia Ecumerium/Citadel, and again
  in the Votha Krr scene.
- **Steps:**
  1. New session, walk east through Grondia until a portrait conversation scene opens (speaker chips
     `Jean` / `Gorran`, `📜 LOG (n)` button, **no** CONTINUE button).
  2. Read the footer hint: `▾ Click, Space or Enter to continue`.
  3. Press Enter. Press Space.
- **Expected:** the scene advances, as the hint states.
- **Actual:** nothing happens. Only a mouse click advances.
- **Evidence:**
  - Default focus state is `BUTTON | HOLD TO SKIP SCENE`. Enter → `ENTER_changed: false`;
    Space → `SPACE_changed: false` (dialog body byte-identical before and after).
  - Retested with focus moved off the button onto the dialog container
    (`activeElement: DIV.modal-content`): Enter + Space → `keysOnDialogContainer_changed: false`.
  - A real pointer click at the prose centre → `CLICK_changed: true`, next line rendered
    (`"Gorran led Jean into the Citadel…"`).
  - Tabbable controls inside that dialog are only `Hold to skip the rest of this scene` and `📜 LOG (5)`.
- **Impact:** a keyboard-only or switch-access player can *skip* a conversation but cannot read it at
  their own pace. The hint text is 1/3 accurate and actively misleads.
- **Confidence:** reproduced four times (two key presses × two focus states), plus a positive control.

### F2 — An open move-category panel swallows clicks on the other category tabs (#557 regression)

- **Severity: High**
- **Location:** combat, any tile. Observed at Grondelith Mineral Pools.
- **Steps:**
  1. Enter combat. 2. Click `OFFENSIVE`. 3. With the panel open, click `DEFENSIVE`.
- **Expected:** the panel switches to DEFENSIVE MOVES.
- **Actual:** the panel stays on OFFENSIVE MOVES.
- **Evidence:**
  - `DEFENSIVE` button box centre = CSS (343, 334). With the panel open,
    `document.elementFromPoint(343,334)` → `DIV | "13 beats"` (part of the Attack move card);
    `defTabIsHitTarget: false`.
  - **Real pointer click** (not a scripted `.click()`) at that point via the `computer` tool:
    `switchedToDefensive: false`, `stillOffensive: true`, `panelHeading: "OFFENSIVE MOVES"`.
  - Positive control: after closing the panel with its `✕`,
    `document.elementFromPoint(343,334)` → `BUTTON | "DEFENSIVE"`, `defClickableAfterClose: true`.
- **Impact:** friction rather than soft-lock — the panel has a `✕`, so the escape route exists — but a
  player who does not notice it will believe the tabs are dead.
- **Confidence:** reproduced twice with a clean before/after hit-test.

### F3 — Every button in the target picker has the same accessible name

- **Severity: High** (WCAG 2.4.6 / 4.1.2)
- **Location:** combat → any move requiring a target → `🎯 SELECT TARGET`.
- **Expected:** each button identifies its target.
- **Actual:** five buttons, all named `ADVANCE` (351×44 each). Target name, distance, hostility and HP
  live in sibling text nodes that are not part of the accessible name and carry no
  `aria-labelledby`/`aria-describedby` link.
- **Evidence:** picker body reads
  `Slime Ibsodsaplevyn 9 ft ⚔️ HOSTILE HP: 20/20 ADVANCE | Slime Erceedro 9 ft ⚔️ HOSTILE HP: 20/20 ADVANCE | …`
  while the button list is `[ADVANCE, ADVANCE, ADVANCE, ADVANCE, ADVANCE]`.
- **Impact:** a screen-reader user tabbing the picker hears "ADVANCE button" five times and must guess.
- **Fix shape:** `aria-label="Advance on Slime Erceedro, 9 feet, hostile, 20 of 20 HP"`.
- **Confidence:** seen once, but it is a static structural fact.

### F4 — At 375 px the combat screen renders seven controls outside the viewport with no way to scroll to them

- **Severity: High** (mobile-playable is pillar 5; T4 owns the detailed phone audit)
- **Location:** combat, viewport 375×812.
- **Evidence:**
  - `document.documentElement.scrollWidth === 375` and `horizontalOverflow: false` — the page does
    **not** scroll sideways.
  - Yet `MAIN` measures `w=501, right=509` and `HEADER` `w=497, right=507`.
  - Controls with `right > innerWidth`: `Feedback` (right 409 — ~41 px of 75 visible),
    `Account` (right 492 — fully off-screen), `Overview` (right >375), `Enemies (4)`, `Follow`,
    `Fit Fight`, `Open combat glossary`.
  - Combat category tabs shrink from 113×62 (desktop) to **68×37** — below the 44 px pillar exactly
    where touch matters.
  - `▾ HIDE` 35×13; `Open combat glossary` 18×18 (fails even WCAG 2.5.8 AA's 24×24).
- **Impact:** on a phone the player cannot reach the battlefield view controls or the glossary. Fighting
  still works (tabs and move cards stay inside 375 px), so this is High, not Critical.
- **Confidence:** measured once, deterministic layout.

### F5 — 19 of 20 Settings controls are below the 44 px touch target

- **Severity: High** (explicit pillar violation; Settings is the first modal a mobile player opens)
- **Measured at 1009 px desktop:**

| Control | w × h |
|---|---|
| `✕` | 26 × 41 |
| Mute music | 31 × 28 |
| **Music volume (range)** | 286 × **16** |
| Mute sound effects | 31 × 28 |
| **Sound effects volume (range)** | 286 × **16** |
| 0.5x / 0.75x / 1x / 1.5x / 2x | 64 × 32 (each) |
| SLOW / NORMAL / FAST / FASTER / INSTANT | 64 × 31 (each) |
| Auto-advance story | 38 × 28 |
| Square battlefield cells | 38 × 28 |
| Beat timeline | 31 × 28 |
| Reduced motion | 38 × 28 |
| CLOSE | 74 × 44 ✔ (the only pass) |

- The two 16 px-tall range inputs fail WCAG 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum, AA, 24×24) outright.
- **Credit where due:** the *semantics* of this modal are exemplary — `aria-pressed` on all six toggles
  and all ten segmented buttons, visible `ON`/`OFF` text, and a real Reduced-motion option. Only the
  geometry is wrong.

### F6 — The minimap legend documents a symbol the map never draws

- **Severity: Low**
- **Evidence:** legend markup is
  `<span style="color: rgb(255,170,0)">©</span> = You`, but the player cell is
  `<div title="Your Position" … color: rgb(0,0,0); background-color: rgb(0,255,136)">✦</div>`.
  A DOM sweep of all 177 titled map cells found glyphs `.` (168) and `✦` (1). No `©` anywhere.
- **Impact:** the first line of the legend is wrong; a player hunting for `©` will not find it.

### F7 — The `✕` close control is under-sized and glyph-named in four modals

- **Severity: Low** (systemic)
- **Measured:** Journal `26×41`, Settings `26×41`, Interact `26×41`, Select Target `26×41` — accessible
  name in every case is the literal character `✕`.
- **Counter-example in the same codebase:** the Feedback modal's `✕` is **44×44**. The correct pattern
  already exists; the other four modals just don't use it.
- Mitigation: Journal/Settings also expose a `CLOSE` button at 74×44, so this is polish, not a blocker.

### F8 — #538's pacing complaint is unaddressed for conversation scenes

- **Severity: Low** (pacing)
- **Evidence:**
  - Advancing the Votha Krr Citadel scene took **~72 discrete clicks**.
  - Shortest single-modal body measured: **`"Humans."` — 7 characters**. Others: `"No. I didn't."`,
    `"Then perhaps that is a question for the road."`
  - **Four different advance affordances** across one session: `CONTINUE` (opening narration),
    `BEGIN` (beta notice), `ENTER` (Passage-to-Mineral-Pools object dialog), and an unlabelled
    click-anywhere with no button at all (conversation scenes).
  - Stacked dismiss affordances persist: `HOLD TO SKIP SCENE` + `📜 LOG (n)` + the advance affordance.
- **Fixed half:** the journal now records all five beta objectives verbatim under OBJECTIVES, so the
  "no journal entry for instructions the player must remember" half of #538 is closed.

### F9 — Story dialog prose runs to ~112 characters per line

- **Severity: Low** (pillar: "readable line lengths in dialogue panels")
- **Measured:** prose column 800 px wide (754 px inner), 13 px monospace, line-height 19.5 px →
  **~112 characters per line** (measured with a font-metric probe, not estimated).
  Typographic comfort is 45–75; WCAG 1.4.8 (AAA) caps at 80.

### F10 — The story dialog's scrolling prose region is unreachable by keyboard

- **Severity: Low** (WCAG 2.1.1)
- **Evidence:** on the beta-notice stage the prose container measured `clientHeight 450`,
  `scrollHeight 599` → **149 px hidden**. The container has `tabindex: null`, no `role`, and is not in
  the tab order. With focus on the dialog container, dispatching `ArrowDown`, `PageDown`, `End` and
  `Space` left `scrollTop` unchanged at 132.
- Mitigated in practice because the typewriter auto-scrolls as it types, but a player who scrolls up
  cannot get back down without a mouse.

### F11 — Focus is dropped to `<body>` when a story dialog closes

- **Severity: Low** (WCAG 2.4.3)
- **Evidence:** after the opening scene's `BEGIN`, `document.activeElement` = `BODY`.
- **Counter-example:** the Journal restores focus correctly — after `CLOSE`,
  `activeElement` = `BUTTON | "Open journal"`. The story dialog opens programmatically so there is no
  launching control, but it should still land focus on the room panel or `main`.

### F12 — Three text elements fail AA contrast

- **Severity: Low**
- Measured in-page against resolved backgrounds across 38 unique text samples (35 pass):

| Text | fg on bg | size | ratio | needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Overview` (selected battlefield tab) | `#ffffff` on `#ffaa00` | 12 px bold | **1.91:1** | 4.5 |
| `Turn order — beats until each action resolves` | `#666666` on `#0a0a0a` | 9 px | **3.45:1** | 4.5 |
| `▾ Hide` | `#666666` on `#0a0a0a` | 8.8 px | **3.45:1** | 4.5 |

- Separately: the combat HUD uses 8.8 / 9 / 9.6 / 9.92 / 10 / 11 px type throughout. Even where
  contrast passes, sub-10 px is hard to read, and at 375 px it is worse.
- The retro palette itself is applied consistently (`#00ff88` lime, `#00ccff` cyan, `#ffaa00` orange,
  `#00ddaa` teal on `#0a0a0a`) and passes everywhere else. No stray hard-coded hex was found fighting
  the tokens.

### F13 — Combat category tabs expose no selected state

- **Severity: Low**
- **Evidence:** `OFFENSIVE / MANEUVER / INVENTORY / MISC / DEFENSIVE` all return
  `role: null, aria-pressed: null, aria-selected: null, aria-expanded: null`. Each has its own identity
  hue (`#ff4444` / `#ffeeaa` / `#00ff88` / `#888888` / `#ffaa00` at 10 % alpha) but that encodes
  *category*, not *open*. Compare Settings, which does this correctly.

### F14 — Settings segmented controls signal selection by green-on-green only

- **Severity: Low** (pillar: nothing by colour alone — sighted users only)
- **Evidence:** selected `1x` / `NORMAL` = `background #00ff88`; unselected `0.5x` / `SLOW` /
  `FAST` = `background #00cc66`. Both carry `color: #000` and the same `1px solid #000` border.
  Contrast **between the two state colours is 1.66:1**. No border, weight, or glyph difference.
- Screen-reader users are fine (`aria-pressed` is correct); low-vision and colour-deficient sighted
  users are not.

### F15 — Damage is never stated in the combat log

- **Severity: Low** (pillar 3: "every number is explainable and the UI can show every roll")
- **Evidence:** a full mace hit produced
  `[13:45:42] Jean uses Attack! … Slime Ibsodsaplevyn exploded into fragments of light!` —
  a regex sweep for `\d+ (damage|dmg|HP)` over the log tail returned **false**.
- If damage is shown, it is only as a transient battlefield animation: not reviewable after the fact
  and not available to a screen reader.
- **Caveat:** I could not observe transient float-up numbers reliably from the DOM, so treat this as
  "not present in the persistent log" rather than "never displayed".

### F16 — Battlefield tokens do not distinguish enemy types

- **Severity: Low**
- **Evidence:** tokens rendered `J ★ S S₂ S₃ S₄`. `S₂` is `Stone Creature Setchipaw: 60/60 HP` — three
  times the HP of the slimes — but is visually identical to them; the subscript is only an occurrence
  counter.
- **Credit:** each token *does* carry a `title` with name and HP
  (`"Stone Creature Setchipaw: 60/60 HP"`), so the mapping exists on hover and for a screen reader.
  The gap is purely at a glance.
- Related: enemy names are procedurally generated strings (`Epficaip`, `Ibsodsaplevyn`, `Erceedro`,
  `Tariepduq`). Four near-identical unpronounceable names in one fight make the log hard to follow.

### F17 — The minimap dumps 169 coordinate strings into the accessibility tree

- **Severity: Low**
- **Evidence:** 177 elements in `<aside>` carry a `title`; 168 of them are unvisited `.` cells whose
  entire accessible content is `"(-5, -4)"`, `"(-4, -4)"`, … They are `<div>`s, so they are not
  focusable — but they are announced in browse mode.
- The `Exits: south` line already provides the real text equivalent, so `aria-hidden="true"` on the grid
  would be the clean fix.

### F18 — One tile title renders as an unspaced identifier

- **Severity: Low**
- **Location:** Grondelith Mineral Pools, the tile south of Grondelith Entry.
- **Actual:** `GRONDELITHDEEPWEST`. **Expected:** `GRONDELITH DEEP WEST`, matching neighbours
  `GRONDELITH ENTRY`, `GRONDELITH ENTRANCE`, `GATE WEST`.

### F19 — The beta notice points the player at the wrong part of the UI

- **Severity: Low**
- **Text:** "Use the Feedback button (**left panel**) to record anything worth reporting."
- **Actual:** the Feedback button lives in the **top banner** at `x=377, y=20, 75×44`. The left panel
  contains ATTRIBUTES / PARTY / INVENTORY / SKILLS / COMMANDS / INTERACT and no Feedback control.

### F20 — "Hold to skip the rest of this scene" has no state explanation and no hold feedback

- **Severity: Low**
- **Disabled state:** `disabled` with `aria-disabled: null`, `title: ""`, no `aria-describedby`. The only
  cues are `opacity: 0.5`, `color: #888888` and `cursor: not-allowed` — the first two are colour/visual
  only, and the blended result is roughly 2.2:1 against the dialog ground. Native `disabled` does at
  least reach screen readers as "unavailable".
- **Hold behaviour:** during a 2.4 s synthetic pointer hold (pointerdown + mousedown, sampled every
  300 ms) the label never changed, no `aria-valuenow` appeared, and the scene did not skip.
- **Confidence:** low on the hold half — synthetic pointer events may not satisfy a
  `setPointerCapture`-based handler. **Worth a hardware-input retest by another tester.** The
  missing disabled-state explanation is certain.

### F21 — Minor semantic gaps in Settings

- **Severity: Low**
- Volume sliders have no `aria-valuetext`: a screen reader announces `0.5` where the visible label says
  `50%`.
- `Mute music` carries `aria-pressed="false"` while its visible text reads `ON` — the label names the
  *action* and the text names the *feature state*, so the two invert each other. `Beat timeline`
  (`aria-pressed="true"` / text `ON`) uses the opposite convention. Pick one.

## 6. Non-bug observations

- **Tested and cleared, so nobody re-investigates it:** my scripted walker appeared to move Jean between
  tiles while a story modal was open. It is **not** a click-through bug. The overlay has
  `pointer-events: auto`, `z-index: 3000`, and `document.elementFromPoint()` at a movement button's
  centre returns the overlay, not the button (`moveBtnIsHitTarget: false`). Only JS-dispatched
  `.click()` bypassed it. Real players are correctly blocked.
- **The Feedback modal is the reference implementation** for this codebase: 16 px inputs, 44×44 `✕`,
  `aria-label` on every field, a required-field marker that is both `*` and the `required` attribute,
  and an honest error message. Every other modal should be measured against it.
- **The combat information design is genuinely strong.** Move cards state fatigue cost, beat cost,
  `⛔ LOCKED` and *why* (`⚠ Enemy out of range (too far)`, `⚠ Requires a crossbow`). The Enemies panel
  states what each enemy is doing and when it resolves. The glossary is categorised and written in
  plain language. The turn-order strip is textual with per-actor beat offsets. This is pillar 3 done
  well; the gaps are geometry and ARIA state, not information.
- **Non-colour encoding is broadly respected.** Minimap legend glyphs are real characters; hostility is
  `⚔️ HOSTILE` in words; move locks are `⛔` + reason text; scroll affordances are `▲ scroll ▲` /
  `▼ scroll ▼`; battlefield tokens use letters and subscripts. The two lapses are Settings segmented
  selection (F14) and combat tab selection (F13).
- **Heading structure is thin but valid:** exploration is `H1 Heart of Virtue - Exploration` /
  `H2 World Map` / `H2 ✨ STORY`; combat is `H1 Heart of Virtue - Combat` / `H2 Battlefield Map`. The
  move panel, combat log and Tactical Advisor have no headings, so there are no landmarks to jump
  between in combat.
- **No random encounters in ~50 moves across the Grondia city map.** The city reads as a safe zone and
  the first fight came from the authored Mineral Pools spawner. Consistent with the map data; noting it
  only so nobody files "encounters are broken".
- **`Exits:` can contradict the prose.** At Grondelith Entrance the description says "A descending
  passage leads southwest toward the Grondelith Mineral Pools" while `Exits: northeast` — the passage is
  an INTERACT object, not a compass exit. This matches CLAUDE.md's "dead end with an interactable is a
  puzzle" guidance, so it is **not** filed as a bug, but a one-word hint that objects can be exits would
  save a player a lap of the map.
- **Primer drift:** `/api/player/full-state` returns 404 on this build (as do `/api/world/look` and
  `/api/world/current`). `/api/status` and `/api/debug/player` work.
- Typewriter pacing at the default `NORMAL` text speed: ~3.3 s to render a 700-character stage. Clicking
  once completes the typewriter and a second click advances — good behaviour, and worth documenting in
  the hint text, which currently mentions neither.
