## Findings

### 1. Enter submits a sole CONTINUE option while the LOG view is open
- Severity: Medium. Location: any event stage that ends in a single CONTINUE (for example Ch02GuideToCitadel).
- Steps: 1) log in and walk to (7,5); 2) advance until the only button is CONTINUE; 3) click "📜 LOG (5)" (the log shows `[1]..[4]`); 4) blur focus (`document.activeElement` = BODY); 5) press Enter.
- Expected: nothing (the player is reading history). Actual: the CONTINUE choice is submitted. The dialog leaves the log and shows the next stage (LOG (6), "Gorran led Jean to a large chamber…").
- Cause (read): `frontend/src/components/EventDialog.jsx:299-342`. The document keydown handler gates only on `showInput`/`isSubmitting`, never on `showHistory`, so `isSoleOption` → `handleChoiceSelect` fires. Number keys 1-9 would pick choices the same way. ConversationStage's own advance is correctly paused (`paused={showHistory}`, line 723).
- Confidence: high.

### 2. Tactical Advisor treats King Slime's Rest as a lethal incoming hit
- Severity: Medium. Location: King Slime fight, (2,6).
- Evidence: suggestion text at hp=110, King Slime 36 HP: "Attack(85) Heat is neutral (1.1×); Attack lands at baseline damage. Rest (potentially lethal) lands in 1 beat(s), too soon for Dodge/Parry to meet it."
- Expected: a Rest is not a threat. Actual: the advisor's threat model labels it "potentially lethal". Confidence: high (verbatim).

### 3. Jambo (Grondia tent) accepts and elaborates being at the river camp

## UX review
- **Keyboard:** staged dialogue is solid (Enter/Space advance, paused under LOG). The CONTINUE path leaks through LOG (Finding 1). After a LOG click, focus lands on the "↩ BACK" toggle, so the next Enter toggles back *and* the document handler may act. The LOG/BACK button also changes position between states (y≈83–106 in screen coords), so a second click misses.
- **Discoverability:** Swap Weapon is absent from every move category (MISC lists Check, Wait, Rest, Shoot Bow, Crusader's Oath) and lives only in INVENTORY → WEAPONS ("⚔ Draw Shortsword · 3 beats"). It's clear once found, but a player looking at MISC/OFFENSIVE won't find it. Some pointer to it would help.
- **Accessibility:** several ✕ close buttons have no `aria-label` (Story dialog, Interact detail, move panel). Enabled move cards carry `title=""`, and the accessibility tree reported them with empty names while disabled ones got their tooltip as the name. Combat category buttons measure 43×24 on desktop, fine for mouse; I did not test touch. Lethal has text; state tags ("⚔️ HOSTILE", "⛔ LOCKED") pair icon and text.
- **Chat panel:** at 720px height only one reply option is visible under the NPC bubble; the other two need a scroll with no fold cue in view. The Jambo portrait is a placeholder silhouette.
- **Story dialog:** the "HOLD TO SKIP SCENE" control stays on screen after a scene finishes (e.g. Ecumerium closing text with CLOSE). The final "tile description as story text" after the guide scene duplicates the room panel.
- **Victory flow:** the no-loot victory uses CLOSE instead of COLLECT LOOT (fine), but the dialog arrives ~2–5 s after `combat_active` goes false, which feels laggy.
- **End of beta:** the END OF BETA dialog stacks over the still-open Ferry Landing interact panel, whose ENTER stays clickable behind it.

- The King Slime surge telegraph is well written, and a 14-beat lead is generous.
- Jambo's scripted intro is the best-voiced scene on the route; the LLM Jambo keeps the third person but loses the salesman patter.
