## 5. Findings

### Jean hallucinates an unstated NPC fact into a dialogue option (Liss)
- **Severity**: Medium
- **Location**: `/api/npc/chat/open` response for Liss, `jean_options[2]`
- **Steps**: 1. Login A5 → :5005. 2. Reach Liss at eastern-descent-nomad-camp (3,4) after `LissObservingEvent`. 3. `POST /api/npc/chat/open {"npc_id":"Liss"}`.
- **Expected**: Jean's generated reply options should reference only things said in the current conversation (or established prior lore), not private fields from the NPC's authoring file.
- **Actual**: Option text was `"You said you adore Gorran—what does a stone being do that fascinates you so?"` — Liss never said "I adore Gorran" in this conversation (turn 0, the only prior turn, was her asking about Jean's armor plates). "She adores Gorran" is a line from `ai/npc/human/liss.json`'s `personality_notes` ("She is curious about Gorran to a degree that borders on obsessive"), not dialogue.
- **Confidence**: High (verbatim comparison against the actual turn-0 transcript and the source file).
- **Suspect file**: whatever prompt template builds `jean_options` for chat-open (not located precisely; likely `ai/` prompt assembly or `src/api/services/game_service.py`'s `_npc_chat_open` path) — it appears to feed the NPC's full character file (including `personality_notes`) into the option-generation prompt without distinguishing "established in this conversation" from "background fact only."

### Liss's LLM voice drifts adult/aphoristic across turns 2–3
- **Severity**: High
- **Location**: `/api/npc/chat/respond`, Liss, turns 2 and 3 (see §4 table)
- **Steps**: Same login/route as above; continue the Liss conversation to turn 2 with option "Do you think they bring luck on the road, or just look pretty?"
- **Expected**: A ~9-year-old speaking in "short, direct bursts," no filter, funny without trying (per `ai/npc/human/liss.json`).
- **Actual**: `"They can be both, child. Some say a polished river stone guides the traveler, others say it merely catches the sun's fire."` — addresses the adult protagonist as "child," and the "some say / others say" construction is a measured adult register, not a child's directness. Turn 3 repeats the pattern: `"...the story says it steadies the heart of the wanderer."`
- **Confidence**: High — this is the single clearest voice-rule violation of the run.
- **Suspect file**: Liss's system prompt assembly / temperature or whichever prompt-shaping step is common across NPCs (Jambo and Mara held their register far better under similar phrasing of player challenge/lore-probing options, so the risk appears concentrated on prompts that invite "explain a belief" framing, which pulls the model toward an expository adult voice regardless of the character's age constraint).

### Jambo's third-person phrasing occasionally breaks grammatically
- **Severity**: Low
- **Location**: `/api/npc/chat/respond`, Jambo (Grondia), turn 4; Jambo (camp), turn 3 closing
- **Steps**: See §4 tables.
- **Expected**: Grammatical third-person sentences ("Jambo thanks Jean for...", "Jambo wishes the traveler well...").
- **Actual**: "Jambo thanks for the patience." (missing object) and "Rest easy, traveler." (drops the third-person tag entirely for one line).
- **Confidence**: Medium — these are style quality issues, not rule violations (no first-person pronoun ever appears), so they're Low severity, but they do erode the "always third person" instruction's crispness over a long conversation.

### Devet's LLM responses run longer/denser than his authored economy
- **Severity**: Low
- **Location**: `/api/npc/chat/respond`, Devet, turn 2 (see §4)
- **Steps**: See §4 table.
