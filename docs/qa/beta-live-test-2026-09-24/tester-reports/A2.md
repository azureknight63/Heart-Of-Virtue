## 4. Findings

### Finding #1 — Critical: combat never formally ends after the last enemy dies ("ghost combat"); `/api/world/move` guard is inconsistent

**Severity:** Critical (this is issue #682's family, "last enemy dies, combat never ends" — but far more consistent than the primer's "2 of 4 sessions": I reproduced it in **every single fight**, 7/7, including the King Slime fight.)

**Location:** Every Mineral Pools tile with combat: (2,2), (2,3), (3,2), (4,2), (2,4), (2,5), (2,6).

**Steps to reproduce (minimal, from any fight):**
1. Engage any encounter, kill the last enemy via `POST /api/combat/move`.
2. `GET /api/combat/status` — `battle_state.status` is `"active"`, `input_type` stays whatever it was (usually `"move_selection"`), `awaiting_input: false`, `enemies: []`, but `combatants` still shows only the player with a `current_move` frozen mid-stage.
3. Any further `POST /api/combat/move` (Check/Wait/Flee/`/api/combat/abort`) returns `{"success": false, "error": "Not in combat"}` — i.e., the move layer thinks combat is over.
4. But `GET /api/combat/status` keeps reporting `status: "active"` indefinitely (confirmed stable across 5+ repeated GETs).

**Expected:** After the last enemy's death, combat should transition to `victory` (or similar), `combat_active`/`status` should flip, XP/loot should be delivered without a separate `/api/combat/collect-loot` call being silently empty, and subsequent `/api/combat/status` calls should report "not in combat."

**Actual:** The session is stuck in a contradictory state: the move endpoint says "not in combat," the status endpoint says "active," forever, until either (a) the player moves to a tile with its own live spawner NPC and re-engages (which mints a fresh `combat_id` and silently papers over the ghost — confirmed at (2,3) and (4,2)), or (b) the player deliberately re-attacks a live room NPC via `/api/world/interact {"action":"attack"}` on a tile that still has one (confirmed working at (2,5) and (2,6) — this also correctly resumed the King Slime fight with its already-reduced HP, so the underlying combat object itself is not corrupted, only its termination signal is lost).

**Compounding bug — `/api/world/move` guard is inconsistent, not simply "refused":**
- With a real, enemies-alive combat: `/api/world/move` correctly 400s ("Cannot move while in combat") — verified once explicitly mid-ChannelNorth-fight.
- With the *ghost* zero-enemy "active" state: `/api/world/move` is sometimes **allowed** (200, and the player actually moves tiles) and sometimes **refused** (400, same message) from the exact same ghost state, with no request-shape difference I could find — e.g. at (2,2)→(2,3) it was allowed; immediately afterward (2,3)→(2,2) "north" was refused (400) while a `/api/combat/move Check` succeeded in between. This smells like the guard checks something transient (maybe whether the last processed move happened to leave `awaiting_input` toggling) rather than a stable `combat_active` flag. `/api/world/interact` (take item) is *also* inconsistently gated: it blocked "There is no time for that in the middle of a fight" on some ghost-state tiles and allowed it on others.
- Suggested root cause direction (not confirmed, worth an engineer's look): the world-move/interact guard appears to key off `enemies` non-empty or a similar transient list rather than `battle_state.status`/`combat_active`, so once the enemies list empties on the last kill, the guard opens even though the state machine never closed out the fight.

**Evidence (representative, ChannelNorth fight):**
```
GET /api/combat/status -> battle_state.status: "active", enemies: [], awaiting_input: false, input_type: "move_selection"
POST /api/combat/move {"move_type":"move","move_id":"Check"} -> {"success": false, "error": "Not in combat"}
POST /api/combat/move {"move_type":"flee"} -> {"success": false, "error": "Not in combat"}
POST /api/combat/collect-loot -> {"success": true, "collected": [], "skipped": []}   (empty — loot silently lost)
POST /api/combat/abort -> 400 {"success": false, "error": "Not in combat"}
GET /api/combat/status -> still status: "active"
POST /api/world/move {"direction":"south"} -> 200, player actually moved
```

**Also tried per assignment, none of them clear the ghost state on their own:** Check, Wait, Flee, collect-loot, abort (there is no `/api/combat/end` route — see Finding #3) — all either error "Not in combat" or silently no-op.

**Confidence:** Reproduced 7/7 times this session, in every combat from small 1-enemy skirmishes to the King Slime boss fight. Not intermittent in my run — essentially guaranteed on this build/config.

**Suspected source:** `src/api/combat_adapter.py` / `src/api/services/game_service.py` combat-end path (per `.claude/rules/api-layer.md`: "After victory/defeat, `awaiting_input`, `current_stage`, and `pending_move_index` must be reset — all three went stale once" — this looks like a recurrence of exactly that class of bug, now missing the `status`/`combat_active` transition itself). The world-move guard is a separate symptom, likely in `routes/world.py`'s in-combat check.

### Finding #2 — Critical: player HP reaching 0 is never recognized as defeat; combat and world state both desync

**Severity:** Critical — no death handling at all, and it interacts with Finding #1 to leave the player able to walk away from a fight they should have lost, at 0 HP, with `state: "normal"`.

**Location:** (3,3) Grondelith Deep East (Elder Slime + Stone Creature + 2 Slimes) — reproduced again later against King Slime's "erupts" attack at (2,6) Arena.

**Steps to reproduce:**
1. Engage a fight where incoming damage per hit is large relative to remaining HP (Deep East's Stone Creature hits for ~40+; King Slime's AoE hit for 94).
2. Keep attacking via `/api/combat/move` until a hit brings HP to 0.
3. `GET /api/combat/status` → `battle_state.player.health.current: 0`, but `battle_state.status: "active"`, `input_type` still a normal selection stage.
4. `GET /api/status` → `hp: 0`, `state: "normal"` (no "defeated"/"dead" state, no game-over signal anywhere).
5. `/api/combat/move` calls now alternate between "Not in combat" and stale-active responses (same symptom as Finding #1).
6. `/api/world/move` at 0 HP is **allowed** (200) — the "dead" player walked from (3,3) to (2,3) unmolested, and then healed back to full with ordinary `/api/inventory/use` Restoratives, as if nothing had happened.

**Expected:** Per CLAUDE.md's design pillar #3 ("Fairness over spectacle: no unwarned deaths") and general RPG convention, hitting 0 HP should trigger a defeat/game-over flow (even a soft one — respawn, retry prompt, etc.), not silently continue combat and then silently allow free movement.

**Actual:** No defeat is ever registered. The player effectively cannot "lose" a fight in the conventional sense — they just get stuck in the Finding #1 ghost state and can walk it off.

**Evidence:**
```
last_move_outcome: "...Stone Creature Quaendy lashes out at Jean with extreme violence! Stone Creature Quaendy struck Jean for 42 damage!..."
battle_state.player.health: {"current": 0, "max": 120}
GET /api/status -> {"hp": 0, "state": "normal", ...}
POST /api/world/move {"direction":"west"} -> 200, success (moved from (3,3) to (2,3))
```
Second occurrence, King Slime:
```
last_move_outcome: "...King Slime erupts — a solid wall of corrupted mass crashes across the stone and slams into Jean! King Slime struck Jean for 94 damage!"
battle_state.player.health: {"current": 0, "max": 126}
GET /api/status -> {"hp": 0, "state": "normal"}
```

**Confidence:** Reproduced twice independently (different enemies, different tiles). High confidence this is a real gap, not a fluke — I looked for a `defeat`/`game_over` field anywhere in the payloads and found none.

**Suspected source:** Combat resolution path in `game_service.execute_move`/`ApiCombatAdapter` never checks `player.hp <= 0` to transition to a defeat state (the adapter clearly has victory-handling logic per the API rules doc, but a symmetrical defeat path doesn't appear to exist or isn't wired to `/api/status`).

### Finding #3 — Low/Documentation: primer-referenced combat routes don't exist

**Severity:** Low (doesn't affect players; affects testers/primer accuracy)

`POST /api/combat/end` → **404 Not Found** ("The requested resource was not found"). `POST /api/combat/pray` → **404 Not Found**. Checked `src/api/routes/combat.py` directly: the blueprint registers only `/start`, `/move`, `/status`, `/suggestions/pause`, `/abort`, `/collect-loot`. There is no dedicated `/end` or `/pray` route. The primer instructed testers to try both as recovery actions and to "use Pray once if offered" — Pray is available as a **move**, not a route: `POST /api/combat/move {"move_type":"move","move_id":"Crusader's Oath"}` is the actual mechanism and works fine (see combat log — it costs 1 overexertion HP as a self-buff). Recommend updating the primer/CLAUDE.md API surface notes.

### Finding #4 — Medium: Tactical Advisor (`suggested_moves`) never accounts for elemental/damage-type mismatch, recommending a move that deals provable zero damage indefinitely

**Severity:** Medium (not a crash, but actively harmful advice that can strand a player in an unwinnable-feeling grind)

**Location:** (2,4) NarrowPass, vs. "Stone Creature Fetur" (96 HP, earth-type).

**Steps to reproduce:**
1. Equip a slashing weapon (Shortsword) — I had swapped to it earlier in the session to verify #671.
2. Fight down a mixed pack until only the Stone Creature remains.
3. Follow `battle_state.suggested_moves` (score 80-90, "Attack") every turn.
4. Every Attack resolves with **0 damage**: `"Jean struck Stone Creature Fetur but did no damage! Stone Creature Fetur's slashing resistance of 0.4x scales the blow down, and 27 protection absorbs what is left."`
5. This repeats for 150+ consecutive advisor-recommended turns (I stopped my automated loop at 150 iterations without the creature losing a single HP) while fatigue drained to 0, forcing repeated Rest cycles, also advisor-recommended.
6. Manually swapping to the (crushing) Rusted Iron Mace resolved the fight in 5 further moves.

**Expected:** The advisor already has `damage_preview`/`hit_chance` data per target in `available_options` — it should either recommend Swap Weapon or at minimum not keep scoring a 0-damage Attack at 80-90.

**Actual:** The advisor has no apparent damage-type-vs-resistance awareness, and will loop a player into a fatigue-exhausting, unwinnable-feeling stalemate against any high-resistance/high-protection target if their currently equipped weapon happens to mismatch.

**Confidence:** Reproduced once, but mechanically deterministic — the resistance math (`0.4x` scaling then flat `27` protection absorption) guarantees 0 damage against that weapon/armor combination every time; this isn't a random miss streak.

**Suspected source:** `suggested_moves` scoring in whatever module computes the Tactical Advisor (not located during this session — out of my time budget to trace further).

### Finding #5 — Low/Observation: `Advance` vs `Attack` give contradictory "reason" text for the same distance state

**Severity:** Low

At (3,3) Deep East, immediately after the fight was joined, `available_options` showed:
- `Advance`: `available: false`, `reason: "No one is out of reach"`, `reason_code: "already_adjacent"`
- `Attack`: `available: false`, `reason: "Enemy out of range (too far)"`, `reason_code: "target_too_far"`

Both refer to the same combat state (enemies at distance 8-10) and are mutually contradictory in their prose ("no one is out of reach" vs. "too far"). Likely `Advance`'s reason text is copy-pasted/wrong for this state — "already adjacent" makes no sense when the target_previews for the same option list distance 8-10.

**Confidence:** Seen once; did not chase further given budget.

## 6. Non-bug observations

- The Pools' geode/fragment puzzle (Azure Crystal, Amber Stone, Pale Grey Fragment → place in order blue/amber/grey at the Grotto pedestal) is well-hinted (two separate inscriptions both spell out the order) but I never collected the third fragment (DeepPocket, (4,3)) or attempted to place any — genuinely not reached, not a bug.
- Multi-enemy packs (4-5 enemies) at (2,2)/(2,4) are individually survivable but grinding, especially against a Stone Creature with a mismatched weapon (see Finding #4) — worth a design look at whether the Tactical Advisor should be smarter here, independent of the bug report.
- The Deep East pack (Elder Slime + Stone Creature + 2 Slimes) hits noticeably harder than the surrounding tiles (one single Stone Creature hit for 42, over a third of my HP at the time) — combined with Finding #2's lack of death-handling, this tile is the most dangerous spot in the zone and deserves either a difficulty pass or (preferably) a fixed defeat/retry flow before beta ships.
- Lore/fairness pillar ("Jean fights here WITHOUT Gorran by design") is well executed and consistently reinforced: Gorran's idle text, the narration when he stops at the threshold, and his mechanical removal from `combat_list_allies` all agree with each other and with `docs/lore` intent as read from `src/story/ch02.py`.
- Level pacing: I went from level 4 to level 8 over the course of just this one zone (Pools + King Slime), gaining ~23 attribute points along the way — feels fast, but I did fight essentially every optional pack, so this may not represent normal play if a player fights only what's mandatory.
