## Findings

### Finding 1 — Critical: King Slime combat can end with the boss still alive and Jean's HP at 0, with no win/loss resolution

**Severity**: Critical (blocks the route; the boss is undead-but-not-dead and the quest-critical `AfterDefeatingKingSlime` event can never fire while this state persists).

**Location**: Mineral Pools, Grondelith Arena (2,6). Likely responsible: `src/api/services/game_service.py::execute_move` / `src/api/combat_adapter.py` (no HP<=0 resolution path reachable from combat moves), and `src/api/routes/combat.py::execute_move` / `src/api/routes/world.py::move_player` (neither checks `game_service.is_player_dead`).

**Steps to reproduce** (from login):
1. Play through Grondia -> Mineral Pools main line to (2,6), engaging the King Slime (`POST /api/combat/move` in a loop: Advance into range, then Attack the boss, Rest when fatigued).
2. During an extended fight my own move-selection script stalled on repeated `Wait` for many beats (a scripting bug on my end, not the game's) while the King Slime's `Stone Creature`-style petrify/attack chipped away at HP and drained fatigue to 0.
3. Eventually broke out of the stall and resumed Attack/Rest. The fight's `combat_active` flag flipped to `False` (`GET /api/combat/status` -> `combat_active: false`) while `GET /api/status` simultaneously reported `"hp": 0`.
4. `POST /api/combat/collect-loot` returned `{"collected": [], "skipped": []}` (nothing to collect).
5. Player remained fully mobile at 0 HP: `POST /api/world/move {"direction":"north"}` succeeded (`_status 200`), and `GET /api/status` continued to report `"hp": 0, "state": "normal"` with no `is_game_over`/`is_death_scene` flag anywhere.
6. Returning to the arena tile and calling `GET /api/world` showed **King Slime still present and hostile**: `{"id": "42921e...", "name": "King Slime", "health": 69, "max_health": 560, "is_hostile": true, "keywords": ["attack"]}`.
7. `POST /api/world/events` at the arena tile returned `[]` — `AfterDefeatingKingSlime` never fires because its `check_conditions` requires the King Slime to be absent from `tile.npcs_here`, and it is not.
8. Used a Restorative (`POST /api/inventory/use`) to bring HP back to 68/124 — worked normally, confirming Jean was simply alive-at-zero, not in any special state.
9. Re-engaged with `POST /api/combat/start {"enemy_id": "42921e..."}` against the same (still-69-HP) King Slime. This second attempt resolved correctly: the boss died from a real killing blow (`"King Slime exploded into fragments of light!"`, `"Victory! Gained exp..."`), loot dropped, and `AfterDefeatingKingSlime`/`Ch02KingSlimeMemoryFlash`/Gorran's rejoin all fired immediately afterward.

**Expected**: Jean's HP reaching 0 during combat should end the fight as a loss (game-over/death handling), or at minimum the fight should not silently end with `combat_active: false` while both the player is at 0 HP and the enemy has nonzero HP.

**Actual**: combat ended in an indeterminate state — player alive at 0 HP with no death flag anywhere reachable outside `/api/world/events/input`, and the enemy left alive and hostile on the tile, permanently blocking the tile's victory event until the player manually re-initiates combat and wins properly.

**Confidence**: Seen once in this session, but corroborated by two independent, verifiable facts (the boss's residual live-HP presence on the tile, and the player's own status reporting 0 HP with `state: "normal"`) rather than a single ambiguous symptom. Combined with the code-level gap in Finding 2, I'm confident this is a real, reproducible failure mode rather than a fluke — though the exact trigger (fatigue hitting 0 mid-fight? a status effect? a specific move ordering?) I could not isolate further within budget.

**Source**: `src/api/services/game_service.py::is_player_dead` (only ever called from `src/api/routes/world.py:364`, inside `submit_event_input`); `src/api/routes/combat.py::execute_move` has no equivalent check.

### Finding 2 — Critical: player death (HP<=0) is never checked from the combat or movement routes, only from `/api/world/events/input`

**Severity**: Critical (root cause underlying Finding 1, and a standing risk for any future path that drops HP to 0 outside of an event).

**Location**: `src/api/routes/combat.py::execute_move`, `src/api/routes/world.py::move_player`.

**Steps to reproduce**: Code inspection — `grep -rn "is_player_dead\|is_game_over\|is_death_scene" src/api/routes/*.py` returns exactly one match, `src/api/routes/world.py:364-366`, inside `submit_event_input`. `combat.py`'s `execute_move` and `world.py`'s `move_player` have no such check, despite both being routes where HP can plausibly reach 0 (a combat move directly; a move that triggers a hostile tile event, indirectly).

**Expected**: HP reaching 0 via any route should surface `is_game_over`/`is_death_scene` (or equivalent) to the client regardless of which endpoint caused it.

**Actual**: only event-driven HP loss is checked. Combat-driven HP loss (the far more common path to 0 HP) is invisible to the client and, per Finding 1, can leave the game in a walking-around-dead state indefinitely.

**Confidence**: high — this is a direct code read, not an inference. Whether it's the sole cause of Finding 1's specific symptom I can't prove without server-side instrumentation, but it is definitely a gap regardless.

## Non-bug observations

- The King Slime's `available_options` correctly showed `Attack` as unavailable with reason `"Not enough fatigue"` once fatigue hit 0 — the fatigue-gating UI signal itself works fine; it was my own script's failure to react to it (spamming `Wait` instead of `Rest`) that produced the stall in Finding 1's fight.
- Sacred Spring at Mineral Pools (2,1) fully restored both HP and fatigue in one `drink` interaction — generous, but consistent with its role as the only heal point before a multi-fight gauntlet.
- The docstrings for `DevetIntroEvent` ("FireRing (1,1)"), `MaraFirstContactEvent` ("RiversEdge (1,0)"), and `LissObservingEvent` ("CampFarEdge (2,1)") in `src/story/ch03.py` all cite coordinates that don't match where the events are actually authored in `src/resources/maps/eastern-descent-nomad-camp.json` (FireRing is (3,2), RiversEdge is (1,2), CampFarEdge is (3,4)). Purely a doc/comment accuracy issue — the events fire correctly at the real tiles — but worth a cleanup pass since it cost me some confused re-navigation.
- Votha Krr's Eastern Gate send-off line says "Seek the Echoing Caves to the **west**, beyond the river," while the actual route immediately after is through the **Eastern** Gate. Likely intentional in-world flavor (geography relative to some other landmark) rather than a bug, but flagging in case it's a leftover from an earlier draft of the map layout.
