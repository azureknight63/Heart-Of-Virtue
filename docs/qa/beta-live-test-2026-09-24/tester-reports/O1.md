## 4. Findings

### F1: AfterDefeatingKingSlime prose never reaches the player (High)
- **Location:** grondelith-mineral-pools (2,6) GrondelithArena
- **Steps:** Log in, clear the pools and kill King Slime. VICTORY → COLLECT LOOT → COLLECT SELECTED.
- **Expected:** Its beats show: "The churning stilled. A deep, resonant silence settled over the cavern." … "I didn't know what to do with my hands when they weren't needed." (ch02.py:835-866). These cover the pool cleansing and Gorran's arrival.
- **Actual:** After the loot modal, the first thing shown was the memory flash, "✧ A MEMORY STIRS ✧ / For a moment, there is only silence...". The story LOG's entries were [1] the memory flash and [2] "Jean steps through the path to grondia...". `document.documentElement.innerHTML.includes('churning stilled')` returned false. The only pending event was `Ch02KingSlimeMemoryFlash`. The side effects did happen: Rare Mineral Fragment is in inventory, the arena description is cleansed, and Gorran is on the tile. So the scene ran but its narration was dropped. My guess is that it was emitted while the combat/victory UI owned the screen.
- **Confidence:** Seen once. I did not replay.

### F2: Jambo's LLM chat is off-location and off-voice, and then fell back (Medium)
- **Location:** grondia-jambos_shop (2,2)
- **Steps:** INTERACT → Jambo → TALK.
- **Actual:** The opener was "*Jambo points toward the shallow stone steps, then watches the current turn brown.* The river has run high since the western rains. If you're crossing below the reeds, take the stone steps and keep your pace steady." Turn 1 was "What else can you tell me?", and he answered "*He glances toward the reeds…* The reeds hide soft mud where the current turns. I have seen travelers step where the water looks shallow and lose a boot, sometimes a leg, in the pull. Cross early, with the staff ahead, and do not hurry when the water reaches your knees." He is inside a tent in a cavern city, talking about river reeds. He also says "I", although his authored voice is third-person ("Jambo does not have a potion for stone").
- **Cause (from source):** Jambo has no `_chat_config_path` (src/npc/_merchants.py:262), so he runs on a generated personality plus `ai/npc/human/world_facts.json`. That file is written entirely for the nomad camp (river, west bank, camp NPCs), and Jambo, Votha Krr and the Grondites are not in `known_npcs`. Any Grondia chat therefore gets river-camp context. Votha Krr and Gorran presumably share the problem; I did not test them.
- **Confidence:** Seen once per turn. The cause is from reading source.

### F3: A timed-out chat turn followed by RETRY hammers the server, then the chat closes and TALK silently does nothing (Medium)
- **Steps:** Jambo chat → option "TELL ME MORE." The client gave up at 28 s (console: `[npcChat] respond failed: timeout of 28000ms exceeded`) and showed "The conversation timed out — try again." with a RETRY button. I clicked RETRY.
- **Actual:**
  - The single RETRY click fired `POST /npc/chat/respond` 6 times in a row, each answered `409 {"error":"Still composing a reply — give it a moment.","in_flight":true,"pending":true}`. The 7th returned 200 `{"conversation_ended":true,"llm_available":false,"npc_flavor":"Rest first, traveler; sore legs and sharper words heal better when fed.","replayed":true,"turn":3}`.
  - The UI showed "Connection unavailable — this reply is scripted, not generated." and the conversation window closed within seconds.
  - Pressing TALK again (JS click, then a real click) sent `POST /npc/chat/open` → 200 `{"conversation_ended":true,"npc_flavor":"A brief shake of the head.","npc_opening":"",...}`. **Nothing rendered**: the Jambo BUY/SELL/TRADE/TALK modal stayed as it was, with no line and no toast. The player gets no feedback at all.
- **Expected:** One retry request, not a retry storm. When a re-open comes back already ended, show its `npc_flavor` ("A brief shake of the head.") so the player knows Jambo is done talking.
- **Confidence:** Seen once, but the evidence is the network log.

### F4: The Swap Weapon panel's "In hand:" label is stale after a swap (Medium)
- **Location:** Mineral Pools (2,2), combat
- **Steps:** In combat, INVENTORY → Weapons → "Draw Shortsword · 3 beats". The log says "Jean equipped Shortsword!". Open INVENTORY → Weapons again.
- **Actual:** "In hand: Rusted Iron Mace", with the button "Draw Rusted Iron Mace · 3 beats". `/api/equipment` → `weapon: "Shortsword"`. The button is right, but the label names the weapon Jean just put away.
- **Why it matters:** Against Stone Creatures (0 slashing damage, F7) the player needs to know which weapon is actually in hand.
- **Confidence:** Seen once. Cross-checked against the API.

### F5: The Tactical Advisor recommends "Turn" while a DEADLY Tidal Surge is winding up (Medium)
- **Location:** GrondelithArena (2,6)
- **Actual:** At the telegraph, `suggested_moves` = `[{"move_name":"Turn","reasoning":"No situational modifiers apply; Turn is a solid baseline choice.","score":75}]`, displayed as "Turn 75%".
- **Tidal Surge legibility (a design question the brief asked):**
  - The telegraph is visible: a yellow ⚠ log line, a red "⚠ DEADLY ⚔ King Slime +14" chip on the beat timeline whose tooltip reads "King Slime — Tidal Surge (lands in 14 beats) — Deadly move", and a dashed area on the battlefield.

  - My read: a first-time player cannot learn how to dodge this from the UI.

### F8: The victory level-up allocator lists "Faith" without a value (Low)
- **Location:** The VICTORY dialog after the (2,4) fight (level 4 → 5)
- **Evidence:** The combobox options were "Strength (21)", "Finesse (19)", … "Intelligence (12)", "Faith". `/api/stats` has faith 18, faith_base 17. `AttributePointAllocator.jsx:68` appends ` (n)` only when `typeof o.value === 'number'`, so the victory payload's faith value is not a number. The first-login LEVEL UP dialog showed "Faith (15)" correctly.
- **Also:** The numbers in that list are the modified stat, not the base. Strength shows 21 while strength_base is 19 (before this level's allocation), so a player cannot tell what they are raising.

## 6. Non-bug observations

- **Degraded LLM:** after Jambo's second turn the stack returned "Connection unavailable — this reply is scripted, not generated." (`llm_available:false`, and Jambo's `loquacity_current:1/13`). I stopped all LLM talk there as instructed. No voice grading was possible for Votha Krr, Gorran, Mara, Devet or Liss.
- **Level-up UX:** a dropdown plus number box plus ALLOCATE, repeated per attribute, works but is clunky for 19 points. There is no preview of derived stats (HP, damage), no undo, and the dialog vanishes on the last point with no confirmation. RANDOMIZE sits right beside ALLOCATE.
- **Fatigue economy:** a mace swing takes over a third of the fatigue bar, and most mid-fight turns became Rest/Dodge. It may be intended; it reads as sluggish.
- **Pacing:** AfterKingSlimeReturn has 6+ one- or two-beat stages, each ending in its own CONTINUE on top of the Space advances. That is a lot of clicking for the payoff.
- **Pre-Mara ferry refusal** ("Something else still needs finishing first.") works but is vague. Mara has already told the player the raft is not ready, so the refusal could say so.
- **The end-of-beta dialog** reads well. The "not today" line lands softly after Mara's "we'll be off", which is fine for a demo stop.
- **Inventory in combat** always reopens on the Consumables tab; Weapons needs an extra click each time.
- **The pane itself:** screenshots intermittently timed out ("page did not finish rendering"), so several observations come from DOM reads rather than pixels. I filed no size or viewport findings.
