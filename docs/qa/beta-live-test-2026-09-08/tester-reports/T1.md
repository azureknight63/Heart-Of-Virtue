## 4. Findings

### Finding 1 — Critical: the route to the King Slime is hard-blocked by an unwinnable, unescapable fight at Grondelith pools (2,2)

**Severity: Critical** (route blocker + soft-lock; the entire back half of the beta is unreachable)
**Location:** `grondelith-mineral-pools` (2,2) `GrondelithChannelEntry`; also (2,4), (3,3), (3,4)

**Steps to reproduce (from session start):**
1. Start on the `full` stack (`config_grondia_beta.ini`), Jean level 1, worn Shortsword + full leather.
2. Clear the briefing; walk to Grondia (7,9) Grondelith Entrance; interact with `Passage to the Mineral Pools` → `enter` → `[1] Step through`.
3. Move south to pools (2,1) Atrium, then south to (2,2).
4. `NPCSpawnerEvent_stone_channel_entry` spawns a `CorruptedStoneCreature` alongside four Slimes; the encounter dialog fires; take `[1] FIGHT FOR YOUR LIFE`.
5. Kill the four Slimes (one hit each — Jean does 46). Then attack the Stone Creature.

**Expected:** the fight can be won, lost, or left.
**Actual:** none of the three.

- Jean does **zero** damage to it. `Jean struck Stone Creature Nurenly but did no damage!` on every attempt. Enemy HP stayed at **60/60 across 6+ attacks**, including **two while Fervent** (Crusader's Oath, +30 % strength). Verified through `/api/combat/status`: `("Stone Creature Nurenly", 60, 60)`.
- Root cause, from `src/npc/_enemies.py:521` `CorruptedStoneCreature`: `protection=18`, `resistance_base["slashing"]=0.4`, `["piercing"]=0.4`, `["crushing"]=1.6`, `["fire"]=1.4`. The class docstring states the intent outright: *"Highly resistant to slashing and piercing; fire and crushing break the crust."* Jean's level-1 40–60 slashing, quartered to ~16–24 and then reduced by 18 protection, floors at 0.
- **Jean has no crushing or fire damage source.** The engine's own `available_options` for this fight is the complete list: `Check, Wait, Rest, Turn, Use Item, Advance, Withdraw, Attack, Shoot Bow, Shoot Crossbow, Dodge, Crusader's Oath`. Attack = Shortsword (slashing); Shoot Bow/Crossbow are `Not enough fatigue` / `Cannot use this move` (no ammo, no crossbow) and are piercing anyway.
- **There is no flee move.** `Withdraw` only repositions — withdrawing to 8 ft did not end the fight, and the move then went on cooldown.
- **The Stone Creature cannot kill Jean either**: `damage=22` vs protection 19, logged as `Stone Creature Nurenly struck Jean but did no damage!`. So Jean cannot even lose his way out. Jean sat at 68/100 indefinitely.
- `/api/world/move` is correctly refused (`Cannot move while in combat`), so there is no exit at all. The session is dead.

**Why this is a route blocker, not an edge case** — from `src/resources/maps/grondelith-mineral-pools.json`:
- The only path from the Atrium to the arena is (2,1) → **(2,2)** → (2,3) → **(2,4)** → (2,5) → (2,6). There is no bypass; (2,2) is the sole connection.
- **(2,2), (2,4), (3,3) and (3,4) each carry an authored `NPCSpawnerEvent` for `npc:CorruptedStoneCreature`.** Two of those four are on the critical path.
- No weapon exists anywhere in the pools. Everything pre-(2,2) is `AzuriteGem`, `AmberStone` (`maintype: Special, subtype: Ingredient`) and inscriptions.
- The only shop reachable beforehand is `Jambo's Tent` at Grondia (12,4); `JamboHealsU` (`src/npc/_merchants.py:208`) is an apothecary — `specialties=[Consumable]`, always-stock Restorative/Draught/Antidote — so a blunt weapon is at best a random spare slot, not a plan.
- `MineralSolvent` (the plausible intended counter, 25 % drop) is *loot from the Stone Creature*, i.e. circular.

**Fairness note (design pillar 3/4):** nothing in the (2,2) tile text, the Stone Creature's description, or Votha Krr's briefing warns that a slashing weapon is useless here. A player walks in and is stuck forever with no message telling them why.

**Evidence:** `T1_039`, `T1_043`, `T1_045`, `T1_046`, `T1b_002`; `/api/combat/status` dumps quoted above.
**Confidence: reproduced twice** — two clean sessions, both spawned the Stone Creature at (2,2) (`Stone Creature Nurenly`, then `Stone Creature Yjoj`) and both locked movement immediately.

**Suggested fixes (pick one):** give the beta loadout a blunt sidearm; add a crushing/unarmed option to Jean's move set; put a mace or `MineralSolvent` in `GrondelithRitualChamber` (1,0), which is pre-(2,2) and currently holds only an inscription; add a flee move; or drop the Stone Creature spawners from the critical-path tiles and leave them on (3,3)/(3,4).

### Finding 2 — High: the Attack button stays enabled when no target is in range, and the rejection is completely silent

**Severity: High** (UI action not working; reads as a frozen fight)
**Location:** combat UI, any fight where the nearest enemy is just outside the move's range. Seen at eastern-descent (1,2) High Ledge with the Rock Rumbler at 5 ft (Attack `mvrange.max = 4`).

**Steps to reproduce:**
1. Enter combat with an enemy at exactly 5 ft (Rock Rumbler at High Ledge, after cancelling an Advance).
2. Open `OFFENSIVE`. The panel renders `⚠ No valid target in range` — but the `Attack` button is **enabled**.
3. Click `Attack`.

**Expected:** either the button is disabled (which is what happens at 9 ft — it renders `[disabled]` with `⚠ Enemy out of range — (too far)`), or the player is told why the action failed.
**Actual:** the click POSTs `/api/combat/move` and the API correctly answers `{"error":"No valid targets available for this move","success":false}`. **The UI shows nothing** — no toast, no combat-log line, no target dialog. Beat stays put, fatigue is not spent, nothing moves. I committed Attack **twelve times over 2.5 minutes** at `Beat 1` before I checked the network tab; a human would conclude the fight is frozen and reload or quit. Recovery is `Advance`, which is not discoverable from a control that just does nothing.

**Evidence:** captured response `200 combat/move {"error":"No valid targets available for this move","success":false}`; `T1c_006` (12 identical no-op cycles, `Beat 1 1 standing`, `Fatigue: 190/190` unchanged); engine confirmed `awaiting_input: True, input_type: move_selection, beat: 1`.
**Confidence: reproduced twice** in the same fight (loop run, then a single deliberate click with the network trace attached).

### Finding 9 — High: "struck … but did no damage!" is unexplained, and the advertised damage range is misleading

**Severity: High** (violates design pillar 3 — "every number is explainable"; it is the surface symptom a player will report instead of Finding 1)
**Location:** combat log, any fight against a high-protection enemy

The ATTRIBUTES sheet reports `attack_damage_min: 40, attack_damage_max: 60`. Against the **Rock Rumbler** — `protection: 28`, `resistances: {}` (literally no modifiers) — the actual results, verbatim from the log:

```
[05:34:06] Jean strikes with his Shortsword!
[05:34:06] Jean struck Rock Rumbler Ewohoxuq but did no damage!
[05:34:25] Jean struck Rock Rumbler Ewohoxuq but did no damage!
[05:34:45] Jean struck Rock Rumbler Ewohoxuq but did no damage!
[05:35:04] Jean struck Rock Rumbler Ewohoxuq but did no damage!
[05:35:23] Jean's attack just missed!
```

Five zero-damage *hits* in a row against an enemy with no resistances, interleaved with one 10-damage hit earlier. A 40-damage floor minus 28 protection should never be 0. Whatever the real formula is, the player is given (a) a damage range that does not predict outcomes, and (b) a log line that says "no damage" without ever saying *why* — no protection number, no resistance callout, no mitigation breakdown. There is a `?` combat glossary but it is not wired to the outcome line.

This matters beyond legibility: it is exactly the message a player gets in Finding 1, and it gives them no information to act on. Fixing the message — "your Shortsword barely marks the crust; slashing is poorly suited here" or an explicit `60 − 28 protection × 0.4 slashing = 0` breakdown — would turn Finding 1 from a mystery soft-lock into a solvable puzzle.

**Confidence: reproduced twice** — five consecutive occurrences vs the Rock Rumbler (no resistances), and every attack vs the CorruptedStoneCreature.

### Finding 10 — High: a failed Feedback submission is completely silent

**Severity: High** (the beta's own reporting channel silently loses reports)
**Location:** `Feedback` button (left panel), any page

**Steps to reproduce:**
1. Click `Feedback`. The dialog is well built — Bug Report / Feature Request / General Feedback tabs, `TITLE *`, `STEPS TO REPRODUCE`, `EXPECTED`, `ACTUAL`, LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH severity, and a "Submit anonymously" checkbox.
2. Fill in a title and steps, pick a severity, click `SUBMIT FEEDBACK`.

**Expected:** either a success confirmation, or a visible error saying the report was not sent.
**Actual:** `POST /api/feedback/issue` → **503** `{"error":"Feedback service is not configured on this server.","success":false}`. The UI shows **nothing**: `page.locator('[role="alert"]').count() == 0`, no toast, no inline error, no text anywhere in `text()` matching "not configured" / "failed" / "error" / "try again". The dialog stays open with the fields still populated and the submit button still enabled, which reads as "nothing happened, click it again".

The 503 itself is *expected on this stack* (GitHub token deliberately blanked — the primer says so, and it is the right server behaviour). The defect is the client's handling of a failed submit. The briefing explicitly tells testers *"Use the Feedback button (left panel) to record anything worth reporting"* and promises a credit — so a tester whose submission 503s in production believes they filed a report and did not.

**Evidence:** captured `503 feedback/issue {"error":"Feedback service is not configured on this server.","success":false}`; `T1c_013` (dialog), `T1c_014` (post-submit, unchanged).
**Confidence: seen once** (submitted once, per the primer's instruction). The absence of any alert node is unambiguous.

## 4b. The King Slime — what is knowable

I never reached the arena, so I cannot answer whether the fight is winnable. What this run *did* establish changes the question materially:

- **Jean fights it alone.** The `Ch02` threshold beat leaves Gorran at the atrium arch — *"He lowered himself beside the arch… He would wait here. Jean understood that."* — and the PARTY panel then reads **"No party members currently in your group."** The assignment's premise ("with Gorran, 200 HP") is wrong for this fight. Gorran *is* a real 200 hp ally in combat elsewhere (confirmed at eastern-descent (1,2): `ALLIES: [{"name":"Gorran","hp":200,"max_hp":200,"distance":1}]`), which makes the contrast sharp.
- **XP is awarded at end-of-combat, not per kill.** I initially read `{'exp': 0, 'exp_to_next_level': 150}` after killing 4 Slimes and nearly filed it — wrong: that combat simply never ended (Finding 1). Confirmed later by winning the Rock Rumbler fight, which paid **+94 exp** in the Victory panel (level 2 needs 150). **Corollary: Finding 1 also destroys any XP Jean earned in that fight.** Levelling before the pools is possible in principle, but every enemy on the way *in* is inside the unfinishable encounter, so in practice Jean arrives at level 1.
- **The fatigue economy is the real constraint.** Attack costs **77** of a 190 fatigue pool and inflicts a multi-beat recoil, so the sustainable loop is *attack → Rest (4 beats) → attack* — one attack per two actions.
- **Jean's real damage output is far below the 40–60 his sheet advertises** (see Finding 9). Against the Rock Rumbler — 28 protection, **no resistance modifiers at all** — Jean landed 10 damage once and then rolled `struck … but did no damage!` **five times in a row**, plus two clean misses. It took roughly 10 minutes of real time and Gorran's help to bring down a 48 hp enemy.
- Against 400 hp / 50 damage / 15 protection, solo, at one attack per two actions, with an output that floors at zero this often — my read is that this is **very unlikely to be winnable at level 1**, but that is arithmetic plus adjacent observation, not evidence from the fight, and I am not filing it as a finding.

Someone needs to re-run this scene once Finding 1 is fixed. It is the one thing this run existed to answer and it is still open.

## 5. Scripted dialogue notes

**Landed well.** The writing is the strongest thing in this build.

- Jean's crusader reflex in the Citadel: *"The ceiling ran up into darkness the way the roof of a nave runs up into candle-smoke, and a hand had half-lifted toward his brow before he thought about it. He let the old gesture finish. It steadied him."* Then his engineer's brain takes over — *"Convection, working the way it's supposed to, on a scale he had never encountered. He wanted to find where it rose."* Two sides of the same character in four lines.
- Votha Krr's register is consistent and distinct: *"You know who you are in your hands. That is not a small thing."* / *"But I notice you did not answer where you are going."* / *"Then perhaps that is a question for the road."* The stone similes stay in his mouth rather than the narrator's, and the *"unhurried as erosion" / "the way a canyon regards a river"* pattern does not overstay.
- Jean's non-answer is the best beat in the chapter: *"Jean opened his mouth. Closed it. The chamber was very quiet. / No. I didn't."*
- Gorran carries a whole scene with no dialogue: *"He tapped his own chest twice — slow, deliberate. Then he pointed south… The Golemite equivalent of: I know."* And at the gate: *"His palm rested flat against the stone — one breath, maybe two… Jean did not ask him."*
- Gorran's combat flavour is a genuinely good idea, well executed: *"Gorran reaches up and brushes dust from the strike site. It's a casual motion. Almost contemptuous."* / *"Gorran fights the way stone moves downhill: with momentum, not urgency."* / *"Gorran catches Jean's eye for a half-second. ## 6. Non-bug observations

- **LLM gates are genuinely off.** No chat panel opened anywhere; the Grondite Elder and Conclave Elder served scripted `talk` text only. No generated prose seen. Nothing leaked.
- **The 📜 LOG viewer is excellent** and saved this report — it preserves every stage's full prose, so a player who clicks through fast can recover the writing. Worth advertising more loudly than a small header button.
- **The Tactical Advisor is better than "heuristic-only" suggests.** Quoting the previous cycle verbatim and then naming a move with a confidence and a one-line reason is legible and correct in every case I saw.
- **The `[1] Begin` button on briefing stage 2 was below the fold** and absent from the accessibility tree until I scrolled the narrative box. It may simply render on reveal-complete (the reveal finished during my scroll), so I am not filing it — but a first-time player on a 900 px-tall window sees a wall of text with the scroll chevrons and no visible way forward. Worth a look.
- **Take-flow friction:** picking up a stacked item is `take` → *"How many would you like to take? Available: 7"* → `CONFIRM`, and the now-redundant `TAKE` button stays rendered underneath the prompt. I lost a pickup to this on my first try (closed the prompt with `✕` and the gold silently stayed on the floor).
- **`Shoot Crossbow` / `Shoot Bow` are always listed** with `⚠ Cannot use this move`, even though Jean owns neither a crossbow nor arrows. Harmless, but it pads a five-item panel with two dead entries.
- **Starting gold is 315, not the 300 the primer states.** Trivial config drift, noted only so nobody chases it.
- **`Ch02GuideToCitadel` teleports Jean to (10,5)** on completion, which is correct and narratively right but surprising if you are tracking position — I walked off in the wrong direction after it fired.
- **Autosave 403s** appear on literally every move (known noise, not reported), but they are loud in the console and would bury a real error.
- **Enemy difficulty variance is extreme and driven entirely by protection.** Two Talus Hounds (35 hp, protection low) died to **one hit each** — the whole encounter took 4 actions. One Rock Rumbler (48 hp, protection 28) took **~10 minutes and 9 attacks** plus Gorran's help. Same tier of the same map, an order of magnitude apart in effort. Worth a balance pass alongside Finding 9.
- **Skill XP is tracked separately and shown well**: the hound victory paid `Basic +20 / Sword +24`. Nice touch, and the panel makes it legible.
- **The loot dialog is genuinely good** — per-item checkboxes, `TAKE ALL` / `LEAVE ALL`, and a live carry-weight forecast (`Current 12.1 / 30.5 lb · Selected adds 0.0 lb · Total after pickup 12.1 lb`). Best single screen in the build.
- **Gorran's presence is inconsistent between scenes in a way that reads as intentional but should be checked**: he is a full 200 hp combat ally on the eastern descent, absent from the party at the pools by explicit story beat, and listed as a tile NPC almost everywhere else. Nothing broke, but anyone tuning the King Slime needs to know which of those applies there (it is the second one).
- **No LEVEL UP modal appeared**, as the primer predicted — Jean finished at 138/150 exp, still level 1, after clearing a Rumbler and two hounds.
