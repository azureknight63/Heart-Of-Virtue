## 4. Findings

### Finding 1 — DemoEndEvent is unreachable; its closing/feedback message never displays anywhere I could find
- **Severity**: High
- **Location**: eastern-descent-nomad-camp (0,2) "Water Step", object `Ferry Landing`
- **Steps to reproduce**:
  1. Complete Scenes 1–6 so `nomad_ferry_ready` is set (confirmed set via MaraObservationEvent firing and completing at RiversEdge).
  2. Walk to (0,2) Water Step.
  3. INTERACT → `Ferry Landing` → keyword `enter` → confirm `[1] Step through`.
- **Expected**: Per `DemoEndEvent`'s own docstring ("Fires when Jean interacts with the Ferry Landing passageway (via events_before)... Blocks the passageway interaction from completing"), interacting here with `nomad_ferry_ready=1` should show "The ferry is ready... beyond the river is where the demo ends" plus the feedback-button reminder, and NOT complete the teleport.
- **Actual**: Jean is silently teleported to eastern-descent (2,6) "Eastern Bank" with **zero narration** — no DemoEndEvent text, no block. Reproduced twice (interacted with the Ferry Landing a second time after walking back in; same silent teleport both times, consistent with `repeat=True` never actually triggering the gated branch).
- **Where else I looked** (assignment specifically asked me not to assume a tile): the `River Crossing Marker` object at RiversEdge (1,2) — offers only `read`/`examine` keywords, not a Passageway at all, so it cannot be the trigger point either. I did not find any other ferry/crossing-labeled object anywhere in the camp or river tiles.
- **Distinction from the declared known gap**: the primer/beta-scope doc explicitly declares "no river-crossing completion event / no Mara crossing beat" as an accepted gap — I am **not** filing that. This finding is narrower and different: the event class that is specifically supposed to tell a beta tester "the demo ends here, please leave feedback" appears to never fire at all, on the one object its own docstring names as the trigger. A beta tester who reaches the ferry gets no acknowledgment that they've reached the intended end of the vertical slice.
- **Evidence**: `where()` before/after the interaction, screenshot `T3_004_eastern_bank_after_ferry_no_demoend.png`.
- **Confidence**: reproduced twice, from a cold state each time (walked back into camp and re-approached).

### Finding 3 — Opening a move-category panel then switching tabs without closing it first silently fails (z-index blocks the click)
- **Severity**: Medium/High (looks exactly like a stuck combat UI from the player's seat)
- **Location**: combat UI, any fight (reproduced at Adders Shelf)
- **Steps to reproduce**: In combat, open a category (e.g. MISC), then — without clicking its own `✕` — click a different category tab (e.g. OFFENSIVE).
- **Expected**: Switches to the new category's move list.
- **Actual**: Click is silently swallowed; nothing happens, no error, no toast. `hit_test()` on the OFFENSIVE button's coordinates confirmed the actual top element under the pointer was the still-open MISCELLANEOUS MOVES panel `DIV.game-panel` at `z=100`, sitting over the category nav bar's `z=5` buttons. Confirmed fix: clicking the open panel's own `✕` first, then the OFFENSIVE tab worked immediately on the next click.
- **Evidence**: screenshot `T3_005_adders_shelf_combat_stuck.png` (shows the MISC panel visually overlapping the category bar), `hit_test()` stack dump in the transcript.
- **Confidence**: reproduced once, root-caused directly. Recommend the orchestrator/another tester reproduce once more in a fresh fight before filing at High.
