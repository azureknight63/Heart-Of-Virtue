## 4. Findings

### F-H1 — Every combat opens with the enemy off the battlefield map, and the app asks the player to fix the camera
- **Severity: High** (my scale: *player will misunderstand* / defeats design pillar 3 on every encounter)
- **Location**: eastern-descent (1,2) High Ledge and (3,4) Hounds Warren — i.e. both encounters I fought
- **Repro**: 1. start `camp` session at Grondia (14,5)  2. reach any hostile tile  3. accept `FIGHT FOR YOUR LIFE`  4. look at the Battlefield Map panel
- **Expected**: the map frames the fight on entry.
- **Actual**: the map footer reads **`⚠ Enemy off-screen — switch to Fit Fight`** at BEAT 1 in both fights. The default camera is `Follow` (`title="Keep the camera locked on Jean (13×13 cells)"`) and the enemies spawned at **8 ft** and **7 ft**, outside a ±6 ft window. `Fit Fight`'s own tooltip says `Enemies are off-screen — Fit Fight frames all of them`, so the app knows.
- **Evidence**: body text at fight start, both fights: `... Follow | Fit Fight | BEAT 1 | 1 STANDING | ? | NO MOVES COMMITTED YET | J | ★ | 100/100 | G | 200/200 | 8ft | drag to pan | ⚠ Enemy off-screen — switch to Fit Fight`. After Advance resolved, the tokens appeared and the warning cleared.
- **Confidence**: reproduced twice (two separate encounters, different enemies).
- **Cheapest fix**: default to `Fit Fight` on combat entry, or auto-fit once when the warning condition is true.

### F-H2 — Hostiles are styled identically to allies on three surfaces (the unfixed half of #537)
- **Severity: High** (*player will misunderstand*; it produced a real mis-target during this session)
- **Location**: eastern-descent (1,2) High Ledge; combat target picker; Grondia (15,5) INTERACT list
- **Repro**: 1. walk onto a tile holding both Gorran and a hostile  2. read the room panel  3. open `INTERACT`  4. in combat, pick `Advance` or `Attack` and read the target list
- **Expected**: a hostile is marked — colour *and* an icon/word, per the "state is never colour-only" rule.
- **Actual**: nothing distinguishes them anywhere.
  - Room panel, measured on the same tile: `Rock Rumbler Xeneh is shuffling about.` → `color: rgb(0,255,136)`, `font-style: italic`, `font-weight: 400`; `Gorran is bumbling about.` → **`rgb(0,255,136)`, italic, 400**. Identical, and the verb ("shuffling about") is as benign as the ally's ("bumbling about").
  - `dialog "👋 INTERACT"`: every NPC row and every object row shares `border-top-color / color: rgb(255,238,170)`; the only differentiator in the DOM is the leading emoji, `👤` vs `🪵` — which separates *NPC from object*, not *friend from foe*.
  - `dialog "🎯 SELECT TARGET"`: `Gorran` and `Rock Rumbler Xeneh` rows both `name color rgb(255,255,255)`, `row border rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`, `row bg rgba(255,255,255,0.03)`.
- **Evidence of consequence**: my own scripted pick in the Advance picker landed on the ally — log line `[04:56:40] Jean finished advancing on Gorran.` while a hostile was the intended target. I had the DOM in front of me; a player has less.
- **Confidence**: reproduced on three separate surfaces.
- **Cheapest fix**: one hostility token — red/orange row accent **plus** a `⚔`/"HOSTILE" chip — applied in the room panel, INTERACT list and target picker.

### F-H3 — Buffed core attributes render as "+14" next to "BASE: 10", which reads as 24
- **Severity: High** (misstates a game number; design pillar 3 says every number must be explainable)
- **Location**: `dialog "📊 CHARACTER STATS"` (ATTRIBUTES), Core Attributes block
- **Repro**: 1. any session  2. click `ATTRIBUTES`  3. read Finesse / Endurance / Faith
- **Expected**: the total, and the bonus stated as a bonus.
- **Actual**: measured rows —
  - `💪 | Strength | 10 | BASE: 10` — value `10`, `color rgb(255,204,0)`, 16px
  - `🎯 | Finesse | +14 | BASE: 10` — value **`+14`**, `color rgb(0,255,136)`, 16px
  - `🛡️ | Endurance | +11 | BASE: 10` — value **`+11`**, lime

  The `+` is being used as a *state marker* ("buffed"), but "+14 on BASE: 10" reads universally as *base 10 plus 14 = 24*. The real total is 14 (a +4 bonus). Colour already carries the buffed state (lime vs gold), so the `+` adds only the error.
- **Confidence**: seen once, deterministic (pure render of static state).
- **Cheapest fix**: render `14` (keep the lime) and, if the bonus matters, a separate dim `(+4)`.

### F-H4 — Six interactive controls have no accessible name (all inside modals, which is why the top-level audit reads clean)
- **Severity: High** for assistive-tech users (*UI action not working*); invisible to sighted players
- **Location**: `dialog "⚙️ SETTINGS"` and `dialog "Send Feedback"`
- **Repro**: 1. open Settings from the header  2. enumerate `input`/`textarea`  3. same for Feedback
- **Expected**: every control has a programmatic name.
- **Actual**:
  - Settings: two `<input type="range">` (Music volume, SFX volume), both with **no `aria-label`, no `<label for>`, no `title`** — enumerated as `range:(no name)=0.5` ×2. A screen reader announces "slider, 50" with no idea which.
  - Feedback: `INPUT[type=text]` (Title) and three `TEXTAREA` (Steps / Expected / Actual) — all four report `aria-label: ""`, `label[for]: null`; the only text is a `placeholder`. The visible labels ("TITLE *", "STEPS TO REPRODUCE", …) are unassociated text.
  - Also: the Title field shows a required `*` but the element has `required: false` and no `aria-required`.
  - Good news, for contrast: all four fields are `font-size: 16px` (meets the mobile 16px rule), and the primary screens have **0** unnamed of 21 (exploration) / 14 (combat) interactive elements.
- **Confidence**: reproduced (enumerated programmatically twice).
- **Cheapest fix**: wire the existing visible labels with `<label for>` / `aria-labelledby`; add `required`.

### F-H5 — No `aria-live` region anywhere: the combat log and narration are never announced
- **Severity: High** for assistive-tech users
- **Location**: whole app — exploration, combat, desktop and mobile
- **Repro**: 1. any screen  2. `document.querySelectorAll('[aria-live]')`
- **Expected**: at least one polite live region for the narration/combat log, since that text *is* the game's feedback channel.
- **Actual**: `[aria-live]` count = **0** on the exploration screen (1440×900), **0** on the combat screen (1440×900), **0** on the combat screen at 375×812. A screen-reader user commits a move and is told nothing; they must manually re-read the log panel to learn they missed, or that they took no damage.
- **Confidence**: reproduced three times.
- **Cheapest fix**: `aria-live="polite"` on the combat-log list and on the narration container, announcing only appended entries.

### F-L6 — A developer note is shipped in player-facing Settings copy
- **Severity: Low** — Location: `dialog "⚙️ SETTINGS"`, EXPERIMENTAL → Reduced motion
- **Actual**, verbatim: *"Shorten CSS animations and transitions app-wide to a near-instant 1ms, for motion sensitivity. **App.jsx applies this as the "reduced-motion" class on `<html>`; see styles/index.css.**"*
- The first sentence is good player copy; the second is a code comment in the UI. Confidence: seen once, static string.

### F-L7 — `#666666` body text fails WCAG AA at 3.45:1
- **Severity: Low** — Location: everywhere the dim token is used
- Computed: `rgb(102,102,102)` on `rgb(10,10,10)` = **3.45:1**, below the 4.5:1 AA threshold for normal text. Instances measured: the map coordinate readout `(14, 5)` at **10px**; `BASE: 10` at 12px in the stats sheet; `150 EXP to next level` at 12px; and the event-modal hints `▾ click to finish` / `or click anywhere to continue...` at 12–14px italic. Lifting the token to ~`#8a8a8a` reaches 4.6:1. Confidence: computed, deterministic.

### F-L8 — Primary action labels render at 9px
- **Severity: Low** — Location: exploration left panel
- The six ring buttons (`ATTRIBUTES` … `INTERACT`) compute to `font-size: 9px; font-weight: 700` inside a `70×44px` button (`min-height: 44px` — the target size is fine, the type is not). Uppercase 9px is the smallest text in the app and it is on the most-used controls.

### F-L9 — Modal titles are `DIV`s, so the heading outline is two levels deep and dialogs are unnavigable by heading
- **Severity: Low** — Location: all modals
- `aria-labelledby` resolves correctly (e.g. `":r1:-title"` → `"👋 INTERACT"`, `"✨ Victory!"`, `"⚔ VICTORY — COLLECT YOUR LOOT"`) so the *accessible name* is right — but the target element is a `DIV`. The stats dialog contains two `H3`s for its inner sections while its own title is not a heading. Whole-app outline: `H1` page → `H2 "World Map"` / `H2 "Battlefield Map"` → orphan `H3`s. Also: every story modal's accessible name is the generic **`"✨ Event"`** / `"✨ Event Result"`, so a screen-reader user gets no scene identity.

### F-L10 — Initial focus in every dialog lands on the `✕` dismiss button
- **Severity: Low** — Location: INTERACT, keyword sub-dialog, Victory, loot, inventory, settings, feedback
- Measured `document.activeElement` immediately after open: `BUTTON:✕` in each case (the staged Event Result is the exception — it focuses the dialog container, which is correct). A keyboard user's reflexive first Enter therefore *closes* the dialog they just opened. Focus should land on the container or the first meaningful action.

### F-L11 — Background is not `inert` / `aria-hidden` behind a modal
- **Severity: Low** — Location: all modals
- With `✨ Enemy Encounter` open: `main.hasAttribute('inert') === false`, `main.getAttribute('aria-hidden') === null`, while a `.modal-overlay` exists and Tab focus *is* correctly trapped. So pointer and Tab are handled, but a screen-reader user in virtual-cursor mode can still read and activate the whole exploration screen behind a blocking prompt.

### F-L12 — Disabled combat moves explain themselves only in a `title` tooltip, and one explanation is useless
- **Severity: Low** — Location: combat move panels
- Disabled move cards keep `opacity: 1`, `filter: none` and the same `rgb(224,224,224)` border/text as enabled ones; the reason lives only in `title`, which does not exist on touch and is not exposed via `aria-describedby` (and a `disabled` button is out of the tab order, so a keyboard user can never surface it). Reasons observed: `"Enemy out of range (too far)"`, `"Available in 5 beats"`, `"Not enough fatigue"`, `"No valid target in range"` — all good — and `Shoot Crossbow` → **`"Cannot use this move"`**, which says nothing. Put the short reason on the card.

### F-L13 — Dead enemy tokens persist on the battlefield map
- **Severity: Low** — Location: combat, both fights
- After `Rock Rumbler Xeneh exploded into fragments of light!` the map still read `R 14/48` with the header at `Enemies (0)`; same at the end of fight 2 (`T 35/35` with `1 STANDING` counting only the survivor). Confidence: reproduced twice.

### F-L14 — Unlabelled `COOLDOWN` glyph readout
- **Severity: Low** — Location: combat HUD
- The HUD shows `COOLDOWN | 1 | ↯ | 4` and later `COOLDOWN | 1 | ⚔ | 5`. No label, no tooltip on the container. It is decodable only by opening a move panel and hovering the disabled card (`"Available in 5 beats"`). One word — "Attack: 5 beats" — would fix it.

### F-L15 — Equipped gear is absent from INVENTORY; I could find no equip-comparison UI
- **Severity: Low** (possibly intentional / reachable elsewhere — flagging for verification, not filing as broken)
- Location: `dialog "🎒 INVENTORY"`
- The panel lists `YOUR ITEMS 3 TOTAL` — `Relic ×4`, `Restorative`, `Antidote` — under a single category chip `🧪 CONSUMABLES 3`. There is no weapons/armour category and no equip control, yet the combat log names `Jean strikes with his Shocking Shortsword of Charms!` and carry weight is `10.3 / 30.5 lb`. So equipment exists but is not surfaced here, and I found no stat-comparison affordance. I did **not** check the `COMMANDS` panel (I was locked in combat) — it may live there. The item **detail** view, by contrast, is excellent: type, subtype, weight, value, rarity, qty, `✦ Restores 48-72 HP`, flavour text, `💊 Use` / `👥 Use on...`.
- Two smaller notes on the same panel: the footer reads *"Tip: **Left-click** on an item to see details."* — mouse-specific copy in a build that otherwise honours 44px touch targets; and in the loot dialog `skip — drop all items on tile →` is a bare `SPAN` (`role: null`, `tabindex: null`, `cursor: auto`) that reads like a control and isn't.

### F-L16 — Tactical Advisor is not rendered at mobile width
- **Severity: Low** — Location: combat at 375×812
- At 1440×900 the panel is present (`TACTICAL ADVISOR | ▲ | …`). After reloading at 375×812 in the same fight, the string `TACTICAL ADVISOR` is absent from `document.body.innerText` entirely — the layout goes `COOLDOWN` → `COMBAT LOG`. If that is deliberate, the mobile player loses the only guidance surface; if not, it is a breakpoint bug. Confidence: seen once.

## 5. #538 — assessment (not re-filed)

**Yes. On this build it is still the worst *sustained* UX problem in the beta**, and it is
worse than the closed report implies, because the affordances are not merely inconsistent
across modals — they **stack inside a single modal, and one of them lies**. Measured on the
Eastern Gate transition, one `✨ Event Result` dialog across three Enter presses:

| stage | footer text present |
|---|---|
| 1 | `▾ click or press Enter to continue` |
| 2 | `▾ click to finish` — **while two paragraphs were still unrevealed** |
| 3 | `▾ click to finish` **and** `CLOSE` **and** `or click anywhere to continue...` |

Three simultaneous, differently-worded dismiss affordances at the final stage, with
"finish" having appeared one stage early. The prose itself is `text-align: center`,
`font-style: italic`, `16px`, in a **718px-wide** measure (~110 characters per line, versus
the 45–75 the project's own accessibility rule asks for), and the two hint lines are the
`#666` that fails contrast (F-L7). There is no text-speed control, no skip, and no journal
for instructions the player must remember.

**Cheapest change with the most improvement — collapse the four continue affordances into
one.** A single footer control in the Event Result modal whose label is derived from state
(`Continue` while stages remain, `Close` on the last stage), with the other three hints
deleted. It is one component, no engine work, no prose edits, and it removes both the
mislabelled verb and the "which of these three am I supposed to press?" hesitation that
happens on *every* story beat.

**Second, and there is already a precedent for it in this build**: the Settings dialog
ships `COMBAT SPEED 0.5x / 0.75x / 1x / 1.5x / 2x`. Add a `TEXT SPEED` (or a plain
"reveal instantly") row beside it and honour it in the staged reveal. That kills the pacing
half of #538 without touching a line of the writing — which deserves not to be touched;
see section 6.

One honesty note on ranking: **F-H1 (enemy off-screen every fight) is the worse
*first-impression* problem and is cheaper to fix than either of the above.** #538 is worse
in aggregate because it recurs on every story beat; the camera default is worse in the
thirty seconds a new player decides whether the tactical presentation is any good. Fix the
camera first because it is a one-line default, then collapse the continue affordances.

## 6. Scripted dialogue notes

Only the eastern-gate transition was in scope for me (I never reached the camp, so I have
**no** evidence on Mara/Devet/Liss voice or on #539). What played, verbatim:

- *"The mountain air hits without warning — cold, thin, carrying the smell of raw stone and something older than either."*
- *"Behind Jean, the Eastern Gate of Grondia has drawn itself shut: a slow grinding of counterweights that finishes with a sound like a word being swallowed."*
- *"Gorran paused at the gate as it sealed. His palm rested flat against the stone — one breath, maybe two. Then he turned without a word and followed."* / *"Jean did not ask him."*

That last beat is the best writing I saw all session — it characterises Gorran through
restraint and it earns the silence. It is also the strongest argument for fixing #538 by
changing the *chrome* and leaving the prose alone.

Gorran's in-combat ambient lines are similarly good and stay in register — *"Gorran's jaw
is set. Whatever he's thinking, he keeps it to himself."*, *"A low sound from Gorran,
rhythmic, almost deliberate. He might be counting."*, *"Gorran fights the way stone moves
downhill: with momentum, not urgency."*, *"The stone of Gorran's forearms is darker where
it's taken hits. He doesn't check it."* No speaker-label or emotion-tag problems observed.

## 7. Non-bug observations — things that are right

Worth saying, because they set the bar for the rest:

- **`▸ WHAT MOVES IT` (the Heat explainer) is the design pillar done properly.** It expands to the full table: `GAINS — Land a hit ×1.25 / Parry ×1.40 / Absorb ×1.25 / He misses you ×1.10 / …while Dodging ×1.25 more`; `LOSSES — miss ×0.85 / parried ×0.75 / absorbed ×0.75 / You take a hit ×(1 − dmg ÷ max HP)`; `Drifts 5% toward 1.00× every beat. Clamped to 0.50×–10.00×.` Every other unexplained number in combat should aspire to this.
- **Hit chance IS surfaced before you commit**: the `🎯 SELECT TARGET` picker shows per-target `HP: 35/35`, distance `1 ft` and `Accuracy: 90%`. (Nit: "Accuracy" is also the name of a stat on the character sheet — 97 — so the label is ambiguous about whether it's the rating or the chance to hit.)
- **The loot dialog does the arithmetic for you**: `Current carry weight: 10.1 / 30.5 lb`, `Selected loot adds: 0.0 lb`, `Total after pickup: 10.1 lb`, with `ITEM / QTY / ENCH / WEIGHT` columns and `TAKE ALL` / `LEAVE ALL`.
- **`TURN ORDER — BEATS UNTIL EACH ACTION RESOLVES`** is a genuinely legible timeline: `◈ Rock Rumbler Xeneh next | ⚔ Gorran +3`, with duplicate enemies disambiguated as `T` / `T₂`.
- **Reduced motion is a shipped setting**, and mobile has **zero** sub-44px targets and no horizontal scroll at 375px.
- **Combat pauses indefinitely for the player** — 18s of polling at BEAT 17 produced no change, so nothing resolves behind your back.
- A pending encounter **survives a page reload** intact (reload at (3,4) re-presented the same `✨ Enemy Encounter`), and the engine rejects movement while it is pending (`400`).

**Where the log stops short of the pillar** (design gap rather than defect, so no finding
filed): resolution states outcomes, never arithmetic. `Jean struck Talus Hound Mysdentoc
for 64 damage!` with no roll; `Jean's attack just missed!` after a stated 90% with no roll
shown; and `Talus Hound Leos struck Jean but did no damage!` — which is protection 19
eating a 16-damage hit, but the player is never told that, and "struck but did no damage"
is the single most confusing line in the log.
