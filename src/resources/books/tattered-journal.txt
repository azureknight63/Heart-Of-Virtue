PROSPECTING NOTES
(Keep them dry. If found, read them through.)

DAY 3
Came in after the rains, chasing a seam of white quartz that ran back further than any I have cut. In the night the way behind me let go - only stone enough to close it, quiet as a door swinging to. Water is good. Oil is not.

DAY 11
I have the shape of the place now. A basin in the south where the cold settles, chambers north around an altar of rock with the light coming down on it. My candle is a stub. I let the wax fall along the wall as I walk, and that is my road back.

DAY 18
Found a wooden chest near the eastern alcove, left by some previous soul, and the key in the grit beside it. Nothing in it I can eat. I shut it and hid the key beneath some loose stones near the altar - a natural landmark in this cave. I copied this onto a scrap and left it in a crack up north. If anyone finds this, search there.

DAY 24
Went back to sit by the chest. Foolish, but a man wants somewhere to go. While I had my hands on the lid the floor moved. Not a tremor. Something under the stone shifted the way a thing shifts when it has waited a long while and is done. Whoever opens it, be ready before you touch it.
